Frequently Asked Questions — AFCON Betting

AFCON (Africa Cup of Nations) is the premier continental football tournament for African national teams, organised by CAF (Confederation of African Football). Held every two years, it features 24 national teams. It is the most-bet African football tournament across the continent.

1xBet consistently offers the most competitive AFCON odds with 50+ markets per match. Betway and Sportybet are also excellent for AFCON coverage. Use the country selector above to see the best-rated AFCON bookmakers for your specific country.

The most popular AFCON markets: Match Result (1X2), Both Teams to Score (BTTS), Over/Under 2.5 Goals, Tournament Winner, Top Goalscorer, and Group Stage Qualifiers. Accumulator bets combining multiple AFCON group stage results are very popular in Nigeria and Kenya.

Egypt holds the record with 7 AFCON titles. Cameroon (5), Nigeria (3), Ghana (4), and Senegal (1, as 2021 champions) are the other traditional powers. Morocco, despite their strong recent form, are still seeking their first AFCON title.
