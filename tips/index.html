❓ Football Predictions FAQ

What are football betting tips?

Football betting tips are expert or data-driven predictions on match outcomes — who will win, total goals, both teams to score, and more. SifuKaii Predicts pulls live tips directly from predictz.com every 15 minutes and matches them to the best available odds from top African bookmakers like Bet9ja, SportyBet and Betway.

What does BTTS mean in football betting?

BTTS stands for Both Teams To Score. A BTTS Yes bet wins if both the home and away team score at least one goal — regardless of which team wins or the final scoreline. It's one of the most popular markets in Africa due to relatively high hit rates and competitive odds typically ranging from 1.6 to 2.0.

What does Over/Under 2.5 goals mean?

Over 2.5 goals wins if the match ends with 3 or more goals in total. Under 2.5 wins if the match ends 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, 1-1, or 2-0/0-2. The threshold is 2.5 (not a whole number) so there is no push — you always win or lose. It is the most widely available goals market across bookmakers in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, and South Africa.

How do I read a correct score prediction?

A correct score prediction (e.g. 2-1) means the model expects the home team to win 2-1. The first number is always the home team's goals. Correct score bets carry higher odds — often 6.0 to 15.0 — because guessing the exact scoreline is difficult. Use them as part of combination bets rather than single stakes for best value.

Are these predictions guaranteed to win?

No prediction in football betting is ever guaranteed. Tips are statistical models based on form, head-to-head records, and league data. They improve your decision-making but cannot account for red cards, injuries discovered at kick-off, or other unpredictable events. Always bet only what you can afford to lose. 18+ only.
