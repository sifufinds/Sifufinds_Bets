FAQ — CAF Champions League Betting

The CAF Champions League is Africa's premier club football competition, organised by CAF (Confederation of African Football). The most successful club is Al Ahly (Egypt) with 11 titles. Matches are held throughout the year with group stages and knockout rounds.

1xBet, Betway, Sportybet, Bet9ja and Sportpesa all offer CAF CL betting with extensive markets. 1xBet typically offers the widest range including Asian Handicap, Correct Score, and Both Teams to Score for every match.
