1. Tottenham Hotspur eye Man City’s “Savinho”

ESPN reported (3.7 h ago) that Tottenham have opened talks for the 22‑year‑old Brazilian winger Savinho, who has been a rotational option for Pep Guardiola this season. Tottenham are desperate to add pace after a sluggish start that left them 7th with 9 points from 6 games.

Betting impact:

  • Tottenham win PL odds – before the rumor, Bet9ja listed Tottenham at 12.00, Betway at 11.50. After the Savinho chatter, both bookmakers trimmed the odds by roughly 0.75 points, now at 10.75 (Bet9ja) and 10.30 (Betway). The market believes a creative winger could lift the Spurs back into the top‑four race.
  • Man City title odds – remain short (Bet9ja 1.35, Betway 1.30) as the club’s depth remains unshaken, but the slight drift shows the risk of a distraction.

2. Jack Grealish – From Manchester City snub to Saudi interest

Sky Sports (5.1 h ago) highlighted that Jack Grealish, the England winger who fell out of Pep’s pre‑season plans, is now being courted by Saudi Arabian giants Al Hilal. The Saudi Pro League’s financial muscle is a new variable for Premier League clubs, especially when a player of Grealish’s caliber is on the market.

Betting impact:

  • Man City over/under goals (season total) – the possibility of losing Grealish nudges the over‑2.9 goals line up a shade higher. Betway now offers 1.95 on the over, while 1xBet sticks at 1.90 – a subtle shift that savvy bettors can exploit.
  • Al Hilal Asian handicap – African punters with a taste for Asian markets can now see Al Hilal at –1.5 against any EPL side in friendly‑type contests, with Bet9ja pricing it at 1.80.

3. Bruno Guimarães – Newcastle stand firm, Arsenal wait

Sky Sports (6.2 h ago) reported that Bruno Guimarães is refusing to force a move to the Emirates while Newcastle United hold firm on a £70 million asking price. Arsenal’s midfield still feels the absence of a box‑to‑box engine after the departure of Thomas Partey.

Betting impact:

  • Arsenal win PL odds – before the story, Betway had Arsenal at 6.00. The stalemate pushes the odds slightly higher, now 6.30 on Betway and 6.20 on Bet9ja, reflecting the market’s belief that Arsenal may have to look elsewhere or settle for a less costly option.
  • Newcastle relegation odds – the club’s willingness to keep a high‑value asset like Guimarães suggests they are not desperate to sell, which steadies their survival odds (Bet9ja 2.40, Betway 2.35).

4. Liverpool’s opening bid for PSG’s Barcola

Betting impact:

5. Everton’s Ndiaye on Manchester United radar

ESPN (11.9 h ago) disclosed that Bamogo Ndiaye, the 21‑year‑old forward who broke out at Everton, is now on the radar of Manchester United. United have struggled for goals after the departure of Marcus Rashford, and Ndiaye’s pace could be a perfect fit for Erik ten Hag’s high‑press system.

Betting impact:

  • Everton relegation odds – with a potential sale, Everton’s odds drift slightly higher (Bet9ja 1.85 to 2.00), indicating a perceived weakening of their squad depth.
