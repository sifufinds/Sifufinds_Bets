Where to Find the Live Broadcast

Betting Landscape in Uganda

Uganda’s betting market is regulated by the Uganda National Council for Sports (UNCS). All operators listed below hold a UNCS licence, except where noted. The regulator ensures that promotions are fair, that player funds are protected and that responsible gambling measures are enforced.

Payments are dominated by mobile money services. The most popular methods are MTN MoMo and Airtel Money, followed by Bank Transfer, Visa and Mastercard. These options make it easy to fund an account from a phone or a traditional bank.
