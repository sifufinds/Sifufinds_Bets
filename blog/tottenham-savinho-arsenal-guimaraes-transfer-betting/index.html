1. Tottenham Hotspur eye Man City’s Savinho

Source: ESPN (5.3 h ago)

Tottenham have reportedly opened talks for Savinho, the 20‑year‑old winger who impressed with his dribbling and eye‑for‑goal during City’s 2025‑26 season. Spurs are looking to add more depth on the flanks ahead of a congested schedule that includes the Champions League group stage, the Premier League, and the FA Cup.

Squad depth implications

  • Current right‑wing options: Son Heung‑min, Richarlison, and new signing James Maddison are all sharing minutes. Adding Savinho gives Spurs a cheap, high‑potential alternative that can rotate in Europa and domestic games.
  • Financial angle: Savinho’s release clause is rumored to be around £18 million – a figure well within Tottenham’s budget but still a sizeable outlay for a player with limited first‑team minutes.

2. Arsenal stand firm on Bruno Guimaraes

Source: Sky Sports (7.7 h ago)

Arsenal’s Bruno Guimaraes has reportedly refused to push a move to the Emirates, while Newcastle United remain stubborn on their asking price. The Portuguese midfielder has been a key figure for Newcastle, anchoring their midfield with 8 goals and 12 assists this season.

Squad depth implications

  • Arsenal’s midfield: With Martin Ødegaard, Thomas Partey and the newly signed Kai Havertz, Arsenal already have depth. Losing Guimaraes would force them to rely heavily on Ødegaard and the ageing Partey.
  • Newcastle’s options: If Guimaraes stays, Newcastle retain a quality playmaker for their Championship promotion push. Should Arsenal bite, they’ll need to replace him with a cheaper alternative, perhaps a loan from a Ligue 1 side.

3. Liverpool preparing opening bid for PSG’s Barcola

Source: BBC (13.3 h ago)

Liverpool have been linked with an opening bid for Barcola, the 22‑year‑old French forward who finished the season with 15 goals for Paris Saint‑Germain. Jurgen Klopp sees Barcola as a long‑term solution to replace the aging Mohamed Salah and Luis Díaz.

Squad depth implications

  • Liverpool attack: With Salah, Diaz, and Darwin Núñez sharing the front line, adding Barcola offers a versatile left‑wing option and a different goal‑scoring profile.
  • Financial side: PSG’s asking price is believed to be around £45 million. Liverpool’s recent cash‑flow from TV rights and a new Saudi sponsorship deal makes the fee plausible.

5. What African bettors should watch

  1. Timing of the deal – If a player is signed before a key match, odds will adjust quickly. Keep an eye on official club statements and transfer‑window deadlines (usually 31 August).
  2. Currency considerations – Most African bookmakers accept NGN, KES, GHS, ZAR, TSh. Use local payment methods like M‑Pay, M‑Pesa, or MTN MoMo for faster deposits.
  3. Betting on squad depth – Markets such as team total goals, both teams to score, and first goal scorer are sensitive to new arrivals. Compare odds across at least two bookmakers – Bet9ja vs. Betway, or 1xBet vs. Betika – to lock in the best value.
  4. CAF Champions League implications – Tottenham and Liverpool’s potential signings could affect their chances of qualifying for the CAF Champions League if they finish in the top four of the Premier League. This opens up long‑term futures markets on African bookmakers.
