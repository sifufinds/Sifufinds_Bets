Licensing and regulation in South Africa

South African betting is overseen by several bodies. The Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB) regulates operators in the Western Cape, while the National Gambling Board (NGB) provides national oversight. Provincial bodies such as the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB) also enforce local rules. All bookmakers mentioned operate under a recognised licence – FairPari is NGB licensed, Betway holds a WCGRB licence, and Linebet runs under a Curaçao licence which is accepted but not locally regulated. Choosing a locally licensed operator adds an extra layer of consumer protection.
