FAQ

What is the both teams to score (BTTS) market?

BTTS pays out if both sides score at least one goal during the match, regardless of who wins, draws, or loses. It's a popular market when two attack-minded teams meet, since it doesn't require you to correctly predict the result — only that both teams find the net.

What does over/under 2.5 goals mean?

This totals market asks whether the combined number of goals scored by both teams will finish above (over) or below (under) 2.5. Since goals can't be scored in halves, 2.5 is used as the line so there's always a clear winning side — 3+ goals wins the over, 2 or fewer wins the under.

Should I bet before or during the match?

Both have their place. Pre-match odds are set based on form, team news, and historical data, while live odds react instantly to what's happening on the pitch. Backing a strong pre-match read can offer better value, but live betting lets you react to swings like an early red card or a team chasing the game.

Is it safe to compare odds across multiple bookmakers?
