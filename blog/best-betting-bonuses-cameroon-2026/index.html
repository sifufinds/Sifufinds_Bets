Understanding Wagering Requirements

Wagering requirements are conditions you must meet before you can withdraw your bonus winnings. Here’s a breakdown:

  • Wager x5: You must bet the bonus amount 5 times.
  • Min odds 1.40: Each bet must have odds of at least 1.40.
  • 30 days: You have 30 days to meet these requirements.

For example, if you deposit CFA 10,000 and get a 200% bonus (CFA 20,000), you’ll need to wager CFA 100,000 (20,000 x 5) on bets with odds of 1.40 or higher within 30 days.
