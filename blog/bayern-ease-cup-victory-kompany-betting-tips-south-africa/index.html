Licensing and Regulation in South Africa

South Africa’s gambling sector is governed by the Western Cape Gambling Regulatory Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). These bodies ensure that bookmakers operate fairly and protect players. A local licence, such as that held by FairPari and Betway, means the operator is regulated by the WCGRB, which imposes strict financial and operational controls. International licences, held by Linebet, BetWinner, Melbet and Paripesa, still require compliance with South African laws, but the oversight may differ.

When betting, always verify that the bookmaker’s licence is listed on the WCGRB website. This step helps safeguard your funds and guarantees that the operator must adhere to South African standards.
