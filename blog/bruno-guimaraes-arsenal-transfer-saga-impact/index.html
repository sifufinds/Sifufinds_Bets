2. Why Guimaraes matters for the title race

Arsenal’s perspective

Arsenal sit second in the Premier League, just a few points behind Manchester City. Their midfield has been a blend of experience (Thomas Partey) and youthful energy (Martin Ødegaard), but a lack of a box‑to‑box dynamo has been highlighted by pundits. Adding Guimaraes would give them:

  • Defensive solidity – his tackling stats rank among the top 10 in the league.
  • Progressive passing – average 2.3 progressive passes per 90, boosting Arsenal’s transition play.
  • Set‑piece threat – he scores 6 goals from free‑kicks per season on average.

If Arsenal miss out, their odds to win the title on African books are likely to drift.

Newcastle’s perspective

Newcastle are mid‑table but flirting with a Europa League spot. Keeping Guimaraes strengthens their chances of climbing into the top‑6, especially with the recent signing of a promising striker (see the BBC “All done deals in July 2026” list).
