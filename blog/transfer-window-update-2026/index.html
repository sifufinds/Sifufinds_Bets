Two confirmed moves worth tracking this window, at very different ends of the market.

Dortmund Complete Karetsas Deal

Borussia Dortmund agreed a deal worth a reported €30m base fee, rising to €32m with add-ons, for Genk's 18-year-old Greek midfielder Konstantinos Karetsas. The agreement was reached on 27 July 2026 and completed on 3 August, with Karetsas signing a five-year contract — the largest sale in Genk's history.

Man United's Fourth Signing Is a Teenage Goalkeeper

Manchester United's fourth summer signing was 19-year-old Welsh goalkeeper Kit Margetson, who joined from Swansea City as a free agent after his contract expired, with United agreeing a development compensation package for the Championship club. Margetson kept 10 clean sheets in 34 appearances on loan at Connah's Quay Nomads last season.
