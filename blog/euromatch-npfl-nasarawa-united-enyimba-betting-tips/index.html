Licensing and Regulation in Nigeria

The National Lottery Regulatory Commission (NLRC) is the body that authorises all legal betting operators in Nigeria. An NLRC licence obliges bookmakers to keep player funds in separate accounts, implement robust Know‑Your‑Customer (KYC) checks and adhere to strict advertising standards. When you see “NLRC Licensed” next to a bookmaker’s name you can be confident that the platform is monitored for fairness and security. The regulator also enforces responsible gambling initiatives, meaning operators must provide self‑exclusion tools and clear information on betting limits.
