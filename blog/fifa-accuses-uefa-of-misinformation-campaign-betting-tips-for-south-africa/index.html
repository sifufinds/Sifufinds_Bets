Licensing and regulation in South Africa

The betting industry operates under a multi‑layered framework. The WCGRB (Western Cape Gambling Regulatory Board) governs operators in the Western Cape, while the NGB (National Gambling Board) and ECGBB (Eastern Cape Gambling Board) oversee other provinces. These bodies enforce strict licensing criteria, including financial stability, data protection and responsible‑gambling programmes. Any bookmaker that advertises a “WCGRB Licensed” or “NGB Licensed” badge has passed these checks. International licences, such as Curaçao, are recognised by the South African government but do not fall under provincial oversight, meaning dispute resolution may be slower. Players should always verify the licence badge on the bookmaker’s homepage before committing funds.
