  1. Study Player Form: Table tennis is highly dependent on individual performance. Research players’ recent results and head-to-head records.

  3. Bet on Sets and Games: Instead of just match winners, explore set and game betting for higher odds.
  4. Watch for Fatigue: Table tennis matches can be physically demanding. Players in later rounds may show signs of fatigue, impacting their performance.
