Licensing and Regulation in South Africa

South African gambling is overseen by three provincial bodies: the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). All the bookmakers listed above hold a licence from at least one of these regulators, meaning they must adhere to strict responsible‑gaming standards, anti‑money‑laundering procedures and transparent payout policies. When you see a licence reference on a site, it is a clear indicator that the operator is monitored by a South African authority rather than an offshore jurisdiction.
