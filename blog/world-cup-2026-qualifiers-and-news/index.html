Cristiano Ronaldo’s World Cup journey is nothing short of legendary. Having participated in five World Cups (2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, and 2022), Ronaldo has set numerous records, including becoming the highest-scoring male player in World Cup qualifying history. His presence on the pitch has consistently influenced betting markets, especially in goalscorer and match outcome bets.

For African bettors, Ronaldo’s performances often create ripple effects in odds across bookmakers like Bet9ja and Betway. His ability to score crucial goals means that markets such as “First Goalscorer” or “Anytime Goalscorer” are particularly popular when Portugal is playing. Moreover, his leadership and experience add weight to Portugal’s chances in the tournament, which can be reflected in outright winner odds.

Comparing Ronaldo to Lionel Messi, another football icon, is a frequent topic among bettors and fans alike.
