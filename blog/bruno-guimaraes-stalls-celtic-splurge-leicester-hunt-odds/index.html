1. Bruno Guimaraes – the stalled Arsenal chase

What happened?

Sky Sports reported (24 minutes ago) that Newcastle United remain firm on their asking price for midfield dynamo Bruno Guimaraes, while the Brazilian has signalled he won’t “force” a move to the Emirates. The Magpies’ valuation sits around £75 million, a figure Arsenal fans have struggled to match after a heavy summer spend on other targets.

Betting impact

Arsenal’s title odds have slipped across the board. Bet9ja now lists a 25/1 price for the Gunners to win the league, while Sportybet is offering 27/1 and Betway 24/1. The dip reflects the market’s view that Arsenal’s midfield upgrade is now uncertain, leaving them a step behind Manchester City and Liverpool.

On the flip side, Newcastle’s odds to finish in the top‑four have tightened. Bet9ja: 10/1, Sportybet: 9/1, Betway: 11/1 – a clear sign that the Magpies are now seen as genuine European contenders if they keep Guimaraes.

2. Celtic’s record‑breaking purchase – Kasper Hogh

What happened?

Two Sky Sports pieces (3.7 h and 11.9 h ago) confirm Celtic have agreed a club‑record fee for Norwegian forward Kasper Hogh, sealing the deal from Bodø/Glimt. The exact figure wasn’t disclosed, but sources peg it at roughly £18 million – a massive outlay for a Scottish club.

Betting impact

Celtic’s odds to win the Scottish Premiership have improved dramatically. Bet9ja now offers 2.6/1, Sportybet 2.8/1 and Betway 2.5/1, compared with pre‑signing numbers hovering around 3.5/1. The market believes Hogh adds the cutting edge needed to fend off Rangers’ challenge.

Rangers’ title odds have drifted the opposite way – Bet9ja 3.2/1, Sportybet 3.5/1, Betway 3.0/1 – signalling a tighter race.
