Betting Strategies for County Cricket

  1. Track the toss: A toss decision often reflects the pitch condition. A home team that chooses to bat may indicate a dry, batting‑friendly pitch.
  2. Monitor early wickets: In two‑day games, the first 10 overs can decide the outcome. A 5‑over strike rate >1.3 is a strong indicator of a likely win.
  3. Use weather data: A 60%+ chance of overcast conditions should steer you toward swing‑bowling markets.
  4. Check player injuries: A key fast bowler out can shift the balance. In the England vs Bangladesh Test, England’s pacer James Anderson was fit, giving England a solid advantage.
  5. Value betting: Look for odds that exceed the implied probability. For example, a 1.30 odds on England implies a 76.9% probability. If you assess the match to be more likely (say 80% chance), this is a value bet.
