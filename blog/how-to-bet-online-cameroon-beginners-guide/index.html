The Autorité des Marchés des Jeux (AMJ) is the regulatory body overseeing gambling activities in Cameroon. Licensed bookmakers must adhere to strict guidelines to ensure fair play and protect bettors. Always verify that a bookmaker is AMJ-licensed before signing up.
