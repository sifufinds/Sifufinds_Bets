African Angle: What Can We Expect?

As African football fans, we’re always on the lookout for teams that can challenge the European powerhouses. While England and Scotland are not direct competitors to our African teams, their performances can give us insight into the level of competition at the World Cup. For example, if England struggles against weaker opponents, it could open up opportunities for African sides like Nigeria, Ghana, or South Africa to cause an upset.

Historically, African teams have thrived when European giants show vulnerability in the group stage. In 2010, Ghana’s 2‑1 win over the United States was preceded by a lacklustre performance from the USA in their warm‑up friendlies. Similarly, the 2022 tournament saw Morocco’s historic run after they observed how top‑ranked teams rotated squads in preparatory games.
