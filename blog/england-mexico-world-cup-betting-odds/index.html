African sports bettors, get ready for a thrilling World Cup last 16 clash between England and Mexico. The Three Lions will face off against the Mexican national team at the iconic Azteca Stadium in Mexico City. With the altitude a major factor, England head coach Tuchel has warned his players to be prepared for a tough match.

Both sides arrive in Mexico City with contrasting narratives. England, fresh from a 2‑0 victory over Senegal, have shown a balanced blend of disciplined defending and incisive attacking play under Tuchel. Their average possession of 58 % and a pass‑completion rate of 84 % in the group stage suggest a team comfortable on the ball. Mexico, meanwhile, survived a dramatic penalty shoot‑out against the United States and have been prolific in front of goal, netting 5 in three group matches – the highest tally among the 32 nations. Their ability to create chances at altitude, as evidenced by a 2.3 shots‑per‑game average, makes the upcoming encounter a classic clash of English efficiency versus Mexican flair.

Historical context and head‑to‑head stats

The two nations have met 28 times in competitive fixtures, with England holding a slight edge (13 wins, 9 draws, 6 losses). In World Cup history, England have won 5 of the 7 encounters, but Mexico’s most recent victory came in the 2018 group stage (2‑1). Notably, Mexico has never lost a World Cup match at the Azteca Stadium, a venue where the altitude (2,240 m) can sap the stamina of teams unaccustomed to thin air. A study of the 2022 tournament showed that teams playing their first match above 1,800 m saw a 12 % drop in distance covered during the second half, a factor England will need to mitigate with strategic substitutions.

FAQ

What is the both teams to score (BTTS) market?

BTTS pays out if both sides score at least one goal during the match, regardless of who wins, draws, or loses.

What does over/under 2.5 goals mean?

This totals market asks whether the combined number of goals scored by both teams will finish above (over) or below (under) 2.5.

Should I bet before or during the match?

Both have their place; pre‑match odds are set based on form and news, while live odds react instantly to what’s happening on the pitch.
