2. Celtic’s Club‑Record Signing: Kasper Høg

Squad depth: Høg’s arrival gives Celtic a new attacking threat on the right wing, while Maeda strengthens Ipswich’s offensive options. Both clubs now have more options to rotate, potentially keeping players fresher for the Champions League and domestic cups.

Betting implications: Celtic’s improved attacking depth could increase the likelihood of high‑scoring matches. Look for over 2.5 goals bets to go down slightly (e.g., 1.75 on Bet9ja vs. 1.80 on Betway). For Ipswich, the odds for home win may tighten to 2.20 on Betika and 2.30 on Melbet.
