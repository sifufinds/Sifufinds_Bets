Licensing and Regulation in Cameroon

The Autorité des Marchés des Jeux (AMJ) is the regulatory body that oversees all betting operators in Cameroon. The AMJ ensures that bookmakers maintain fair play, protect player funds, and comply with responsible gambling standards. When you choose an AMJ‑licensed bookmaker—such as PMUC, 1xBet, Melbet, FairPari or BetPawa—you are guaranteed that the platform is monitored and that your deposits are protected.

Responsible Gambling

Responsible gambling is a core principle for every bookmaker in Cameroon. AMJ requires operators to provide tools such as deposit limits, loss limits, self‑exclusion and time‑out features.
