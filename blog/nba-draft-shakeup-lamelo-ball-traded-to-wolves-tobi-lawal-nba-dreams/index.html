The 2026 NBA Draft has come and gone, leaving many teams with new faces and fresh expectations. Among the most significant trades was the one that sent LaMelo Ball from the Charlotte Hornets to the Minnesota Timberwolves. We'll dive into what this means for both teams and explore the world of player prop betting, a vital aspect of the African sports betting ecosystem.

Wolves land LaMelo Ball in a blockbuster 4‑team trade with Hornets

The Minnesota Timberwolves, who have flirted with the playoffs in the past but never quite broken through, have now acquired the flashy point‑guard who led the league in assists during the 2023‑24 season. LaMelo Ball averaged 20.9 points, 9.6 assists and 6.8 rebounds per game in 2024, and he remains the only player in NBA history to post a 30‑point, 10‑assist, 10‑rebound triple‑double at the age of 20. His ability to create for himself and others makes him a perfect fit for the Timberwolves’ young core of Anthony Edwards, D'Angelo Russell, and new draft pick Jaden Hardy.

The trade was a 4‑team deal that saw Charlotte acquire Naz Reid, seven draft picks, and Josh Green. Reid, a solid backup guard, can provide depth on the wing, while the draft picks give the Hornets flexibility to rebuild or add pieces that fit a young core. Josh Green, a versatile forward with a 47% three‑point shooting percentage, brings perimeter defense and a reliable mid‑range shot to the Hornets.

CBS Sports graded the trade as a B+ for Minnesota, citing Ball’s high ceiling and the immediate upside he brings to a team that has been in the playoff bubble for the past two seasons. The Hornets, meanwhile, are looking to retool around a younger roster and focus on long‑term development.

Impact on Minnesota’s playoff push

With Ball at the helm, the Timberwolves now have a true point‑guard who can orchestrate the offense, pick‑and‑roll with Edwards, and guard the best perimeter players in the league. If Ball can maintain his 2024 efficiency—he shot 46.5% from the field and 38.4% from beyond the arc—Minnesota could see a jump in offensive rating from 108.5 to an estimated 112.0. This 3.5 point increase would translate to roughly 10–12 more wins over a 82‑game season, enough to push them into the West Conference playoffs for the first time since 2018.

The trade also balances the Wolves’ roster. The front‑court depth remains robust with players like Rudy Gobert and Karl-Anthony Towns, but adding Ball gives the team a floor‑scrambling guard who can play off the ball and drive to the basket in the same fashion as former MVP Damian Lillard.

The Hornets’ new direction

Charlotte’s acquisition of seven draft picks is a strategic move that signals a long‑term rebuild. The picks include a 2027 first‑rounder and a 2028 second‑rounder, giving the Hornets flexibility to trade for proven talent or to keep young prospects. Naz Reid’s presence ensures the guard rotation remains solid while the team looks to develop a new core. Josh Green’s defensive prowess and shooting efficiency can help the Hornets remain competitive during the transition.

Tobi Lawal: From Britain to the NBA

Across the Atlantic, the 2026 draft also brought home a bright new star for fans in Africa and beyond. Tobi Lawal, the British guard who spent three seasons at the University of West Indies, was selected 12th overall by the Los Angeles Clippers. Lawal’s rookie campaign in the NBA was marked by a 17.6 points per game average and a 46% three‑point shooting percentage, making him an instant fan favorite.

Lawal’s statement that he plans to “live” in the gym and “bid to establish himself as a top NBA player” highlights his work ethic and ambition—qualities that resonate with African sports bettors who appreciate the grind and dedication behind the numbers.

How Lawal’s style translates to betting

Lawal is known for his quick first step and deep shooting range, making him a prime target for player prop markets such as points, three‑point attempts, and free‑throw percentage. His 2024 season saw him attempt 5.2 three‑pointers per game and shoot 42% from beyond the arc, suggesting a solid upside for bettors looking for high‑scoring nights.
