Licensing, regulation and payment options in South Africa

South Africa’s gambling landscape is overseen by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). All the bookmakers listed hold at least one of these licences, ensuring they operate within the legal framework.

Payment options reflect the country’s banking habits. FNB, Standard Bank, Nedbank and Capitec dominate the instant EFT scene, while Visa and Mastercard remain universal for online cards. Blue Voucher is also accepted by some local sites, offering an alternative for players without a traditional bank account. Choosing a bookmaker that supports your preferred method reduces friction and speeds up both deposits and withdrawals.

Responsible gambling
