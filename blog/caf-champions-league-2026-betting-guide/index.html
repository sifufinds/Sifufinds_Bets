Analysis: Al-Ahly — African Football's Powerhouse

Al-Ahly are the most successful club in CAF Champions League history with 12 titles. Their Egyptian-based squad has retained the core that dominated African football for the past decade. Coach Marcel Koller has implemented a more dynamic pressing style that suits their depth.

Key players: Mohamed Sherif (striker, top scorer in the Egyptian Premier League), Aliou Dieng, and young talents emerging through Al-Ahly's academy.

Verdict: Al-Ahly are the justifiable favourites. Back them to win the group stage and reach the final at minimum.

TP Mazembe — The Giant Killers

DR Congo's most successful club and the only non-North African team to win the CAF Champions League in the modern era (2010 and 2015). TP Mazembe bring passion, physicality, and a partisan Lubumbashi home crowd that has beaten Egyptian giants on multiple occasions.

Best bet: TP Mazembe to advance from group stage is value at most bookmakers.

Mamelodi Sundowns — South Africa's Champions

Sundowns are South Africa's wealthiest and most successful club, having invested heavily in squad depth. Their technical style of play suits the CAF CL format. CAF CL is the only major trophy they haven't won — expect a strong run in 2026.

CAF Champions League 2026 — Group Stage Betting Strategy

Home Advantage is Decisive

Unlike European club football where away wins are common, CAF CL home teams win approximately 62% of group stage matches. Away travel in Africa is extremely difficult — long flights, extreme heat, and hostile stadiums create a dramatic home field advantage.

Betting implication: Back home teams at odds above 1.80 — these represent value given the actual win rate.

North African Dominance

Egyptian (Al-Ahly, Zamalek) and Moroccan (Wydad, Raja Casablanca) clubs dominate CAF CL betting. They have:

  • Higher player quality (CAF Player of the Year consistently from these countries)
  • Better infrastructure for European-style preparation
  • Higher budgets for squad depth

Sub-Saharan Challengers

TP Mazembe (DR Congo), Mamelodi Sundowns (South Africa), and Simba SC (Tanzania) have all challenged for titles in the past decade. Sundowns represent the best value in pre-season outright markets before the campaign draws are made.
