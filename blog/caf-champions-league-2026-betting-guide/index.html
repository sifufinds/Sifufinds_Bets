CAF Champions League 2026 — Full Breakdown

Group Stage Overview

The CAF Champions League 2026 group stage features 16 clubs across 4 groups of 4, with the top 2 from each group advancing to the quarterfinals. The competition is dominated by North and East African clubs historically.

North Africa's dominance: Egyptian clubs (Al-Ahly, Zamalek) and Moroccan clubs (Wydad, Raja) have won 22 of the last 30 CAF Champions League titles. Backing North African clubs in away games across sub-Saharan Africa offers consistent value.
