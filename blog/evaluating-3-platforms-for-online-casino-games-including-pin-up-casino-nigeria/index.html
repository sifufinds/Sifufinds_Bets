FAQ

What makes an online casino legal to operate in Nigeria?

An online casino is legally permitted to operate in Nigeria if it holds a valid license from a recognized regulatory body, such as the National Lottery Regulatory Commission (NLRC) or a state-level equivalent like the Lagos State Lotteries Board.

How do loyalty points function in casino retention schemes?

Loyalty points, such as pincoins, are digital tokens awarded to players based on their deposit volume and betting frequency. Accumulating these points advances the player through tiered levels, unlocking increasingly favorable exchange rates and exclusive promotional offers.

Why do operators restrict bonuses to specific payment methods?

Operators restrict bonuses to specific payment channels to mitigate fraud and control transaction costs. Certain localized mobile money options or cryptocurrencies may be excluded from welcome offers because their anonymity complicates the anti-money laundering (AML) verification process.
