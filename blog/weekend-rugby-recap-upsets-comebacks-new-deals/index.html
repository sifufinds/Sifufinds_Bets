Speaking of correct score, it's essential to understand how this market works. Correct score betting involves predicting the exact score at the end of the match. For example, if you bet on Exeter to win 25-20, you'll be rewarded if the final score matches that prediction. However, be aware that odds for correct score markets can be relatively low, ranging from 3.00 to 12.00 (Betway) or 3.20 to 11.00 (Sportybet).

First try scorer markets, on the other hand, involve predicting which player will score the first try of the match. These markets often have lower odds, ranging from 2.00 to 5.00 (1xBet) or 2.20 to 4.50 (Melbet).
