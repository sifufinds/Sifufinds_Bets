Licensing and regulation in Kenya

All the bookmakers discussed are regulated by the Betting Control and Licensing Board (BCLB). The BCLB issues licences that require operators to adhere to strict standards on player protection, fair play and anti‑money‑laundering procedures. A BCLB licence means the bookmaker must keep a local office, provide a clear complaints process and submit regular financial reports. When you bet with a BCLB‑licensed site, your funds are held in a segregated account, giving you an extra layer of security. If you encounter any dispute, you can lodge a complaint directly with the BCLB, which has the authority to investigate and impose sanctions.
