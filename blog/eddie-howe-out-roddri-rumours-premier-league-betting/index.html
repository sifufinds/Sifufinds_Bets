Ipswich’s interest in the young midfielder Henry Lawrence (BBC, 4.4 h) hints at a possible Championship‑to‑Premier League pipeline. Should the deal happen, Ipswich could become a promotion favourite.

  • Championship promotion odds: Betway offers 6/1 (7.0 decimal) for Ipswich finishing top‑two, up from 9/1 (10.0 decimal).
