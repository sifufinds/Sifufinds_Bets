One club broke its transfer record to sign a proven goalscorer; one of Europe's biggest stars decided not to leave at all.

Celtic Break Their Transfer Record

Celtic completed a club-record £11m signing of Danish striker Kasper Hogh from Bodo/Glimt on 29 July 2026, on a four-year deal with an option for a further year. Hogh had been Celtic's top centre-forward target all summer, scoring 53 goals in 102 appearances for the Norwegian club and impressing throughout their Champions League run, including a goal at Atletico Madrid.

Vinicius Jr Signs New Real Madrid Deal

Vinicius Junior agreed a new contract running until 30 June 2032, ending months of speculation over his future that included reported interest from Arsenal and a lucrative Saudi Pro League offer. Real Madrid moved to secure him with improved terms after negotiations had reportedly been deadlocked for over a year.
