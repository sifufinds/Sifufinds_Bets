Licensing and Regulation in South Africa

South Africa’s gambling industry is overseen by the WCGRB (Western Cape Gambling Board), the NGB (Northern Gauteng Board), and the ECGBB (Eastern Cape Gambling Board). These bodies enforce strict licensing requirements and ensure that operators provide fair and transparent betting services. While some of the bookmakers above hold international licences, they are still required to comply with South African regulations when operating locally. This guarantees that player funds are protected and that the odds are set fairly.
