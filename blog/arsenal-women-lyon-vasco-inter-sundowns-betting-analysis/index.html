The Guardian’s live‑blog confirmed the 8 pm GMT kickoff for the Women’s Champions League tie between Arsenal and the French powerhouse OL Lyonnes. Both sides are loaded with internationals, and the first leg is expected to be tightly contested.

Betting angles

  • Match‑winner market – Arsenal have a strong home record in the UK, but Lyon are the reigning European champions. African bettors often favour the “home‑team advantage” narrative, which can push Arsenal odds slightly lower on Bet9ja and Sportybet.
  • Both‑teams‑to‑score (BTTS) – Both squads boast prolific forwards; BTTS is a popular market on 1xBet and Betway for this fixture.

2. Brazilian relegation battle – Vasco vs Internacional

Yahoo Soccer posted the confirmed line‑ups for the Thursday night clash in the Brasileirão. Both clubs are fighting to avoid the drop, and the stakes are massive for their fanbases.

Betting angles
