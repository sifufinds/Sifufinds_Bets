Licensing and regulation in South Africa

Betting in South Africa is governed by a network of provincial gambling boards, namely the Western Cape Gambling Regulatory Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). These bodies enforce strict licensing requirements, consumer protection standards and responsible‑gambling obligations. All the bookmakers listed in the comparison table hold either a Curaçao licence, an international licence, or a licence recognised by the NGB. While Curaçao licences are not issued by South African authorities, the operators are still required to comply with local payment and tax regulations.
