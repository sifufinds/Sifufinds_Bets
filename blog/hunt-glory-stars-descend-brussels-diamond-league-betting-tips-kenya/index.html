Safety and integrity are paramount when gambling online. All the bookmakers featured in this guide hold valid licences issued by the Betting Control and Licensing Board (BCLB). For instance, Betika Kenya operates under BCLB Lic. BK0000679, while SportPesa, 1xBet, 22Bet, Melbet, and HelaBet are all listed as BCLB Licensed entities.

Betting with a BCLB-licensed operator guarantees that your money is held in segregated accounts and that disputes can be resolved through official channels. Avoid unlicensed offshore sites that may offer attractive bonuses but lack the oversight required to safeguard your interests.
