Licensing and Regulation in South Africa

South Africa’s gambling industry is regulated provincially by bodies such as the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), National Gambling Board (NGB), and Eastern Cape Gambling and Betting Board (ECGBB).
