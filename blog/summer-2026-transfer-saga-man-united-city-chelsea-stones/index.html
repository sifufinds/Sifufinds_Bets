Manchester City's summer has produced one confirmed departure and one that is still very much unresolved, while Manchester United have quietly completed their fourth signing of the window.

Confirmed: John Stones Joins Inter Milan

Stones left City as a free agent after his contract expired at the end of June, signing a two-year deal with Inter Milan reported at around €4m a season. Juventus had made contact with his camp before Inter moved, and Arsenal and Chelsea were also credited with interest.

Unresolved: Rodri's Real Madrid Pursuit

Correction, 15 August 2026: this section originally reported Real Madrid as the club pursuing Rodri. That was based on earlier reporting — the move under negotiation is actually with Barcelona. Barcelona have made a second offer worth a reported €65m plus €5m in add-ons; Manchester City want closer to €80m. No fee has been agreed.

Rodri's situation is a genuinely open story, not a done deal. Real Madrid have made contact with Manchester City over a move for the Spain captain, reportedly willing to pay in the region of £43-51m (€50-60m), while City are said to want closer to €80m given he has just over a year left on his contract. City have stayed tight-lipped amid the interest, and Rodri is reported to be pushing for the move for both football and family reasons. As of the most recent reporting, no transfer has been agreed.

Man United's Fourth Signing Wasn't Who You'd Expect

Away from the headline names, Manchester United's fourth summer signing was 19-year-old Welsh goalkeeper Kit Margetson, who joined from Swansea City as a free agent after his contract expired, with United agreeing a development compensation package for the Championship club. Margetson kept 10 clean sheets in 34 appearances on loan at Connah's Quay Nomads last season.
