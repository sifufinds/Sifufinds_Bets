Licensing and regulation in Morocco

The Marocaine des Jeux et des Sports (MDJS) is the sole regulator for sports betting in Morocco. MDJS‑licensed operators must adhere to strict player‑protection rules, anti‑fraud measures and fair‑play standards. If you encounter any dispute, MDJS provides a legal recourse that is not available with operators licensed only in Curacao. For Moroccan punters, a MDJS licence is a signal of trustworthiness.
