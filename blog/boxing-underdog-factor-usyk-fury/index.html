Usyk's World Titles: Will Moses Itauma Take Over?

Heavyweight champion Oleksandr Usyk is weighing his final fight options, and the question on everyone's mind is what will happen to his world titles. Will Moses Itauma, the rising star, take over the division? We're not making any predictions, but we can tell you that African bettors love an underdog story.

Why Itauma Is a Plausible Threat

Itauma’s recent run‑up includes a knockout‑streak of three fights, two of which came in the heavyweight division against opponents averaging 235 lb. His punch‑output per round (≈48 punches) is comparable to the early‑career numbers of Anthony Joshua, and his reach (78 in) gives him a tactical edge against shorter sluggers. While he still lacks the big‑stage exposure of Usyk or Fury, his knockout‑to‑win ratio of 75 % makes him an attractive value pick.

Fury Trilogy Ruled Out: What's Next for Usyk?

Tyson Fury has ruled out a trilogy fight with Oleksandr Usyk, clearing the path for Usyk to explore other options. This decision opens several scenarios:

  1. A Rematch with Anthony Joshua – Joshua’s recent 2‑round KO of Andy Ruiz Jr. has reignited his marketability. Usyk’s technical style vs. Joshua’s power makes for a classic clash of skill vs. brawn.
  2. A Cross‑Divisional Challenge with Deontay Wilder – Though Wilder is now retired, a “legacy” bout could be arranged for a charity event, offering bettors a novelty market.
  3. A Return to Unification with a European Contender – Fighters like Dillian Whyte or Luis Ortiz could be on the radar, providing mid‑range odds (3:1 to 5:1) across African bookmakers.

Statistical Edge

Usyk’s 2023‑2024 performance metrics:

  • Accuracy: 58 % (vs. Fury’s 45 %)
  • Defensive Efficiency: 0.72 punches absorbed per round
  • Average Fight Duration: 8.3 rounds (vs. Fury’s 9.1)

These figures suggest Usyk can out‑box most heavyweights if he avoids prolonged exchanges. For bettors, the key is to look for odds that undervalue his technical superiority—often found on niche platforms like Melbet or 22Bet.

Manny Pacquiao: A Two‑Weight Champion Targeted for a World Title Fight

Manny Pacquiao, the Filipino legend, is being targeted for a world title fight against a two‑weight champion. While the opponent remains unnamed, speculation points to Naoya Inoue (if he moves up) or Errol Spence Jr. (if a super‑welterweight bout is arranged).

Market Reaction

  • Hollywoodbets lists Pacquiao at R 30 / R 5 (6:1) for a win, reflecting the high‑risk, high‑reward nature of an older fighter facing a prime champion.
  • Bet9ja shows a tighter line of 4:1 for Pacquiao, indicating confidence in his experience and ring IQ.

What Bettors Should Watch

  • Age vs. Power: Pacquiao is 45, and while his speed has faded, his ring craft remains elite. Against a two‑weight champ, the fight may come down to a single decisive round.
  • Weight Cut: If the bout is at light‑middleweight (154 lb), Pacquiao’s ability to make weight safely could influence the odds. Keep an eye on pre‑fight weigh‑ins for last‑minute line adjustments.
