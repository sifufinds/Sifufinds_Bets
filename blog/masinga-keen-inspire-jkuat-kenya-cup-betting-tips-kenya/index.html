Licensing and regulation in Kenya

All the bookmakers highlighted operate under the oversight of the Betting Control and Licensing Board (BCLB). The board ensures that operators meet strict standards for player protection, fair play and financial transparency. By choosing a BCLB‑licensed platform, you benefit from a clear dispute‑resolution process and the guarantee that your funds are held in a segregated account. Payment methods popular with Kenyan punters

Mobile money dominates the Kenyan betting landscape. M‑Pesa and Airtel Money allow instant deposits and withdrawals, often processed within minutes.
