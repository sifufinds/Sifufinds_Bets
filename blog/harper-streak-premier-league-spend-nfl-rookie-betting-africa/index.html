FAQ

Q: How does a player’s streak affect MLB betting odds?

A sustained streak like Harper’s forces bookmakers to adjust both team and player markets, usually shortening the odds on the team’s win‑line and lengthening the odds on the opponent.

Q: Should I bet on clubs that spent less in the transfer window?

Not automatically. While big spenders often see odds shorten, clubs that stay frugal can offer value if the market over‑estimates the impact of spending.

Q: Are rookie‑of‑the‑year futures worth betting on early?

Yes, especially when a player receives strong early‑season buzz. Odds tend to drift as the season progresses, so early bets can lock in higher returns.
