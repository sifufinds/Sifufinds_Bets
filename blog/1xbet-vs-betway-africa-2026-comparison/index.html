FAQs — 1xBet vs Betway Africa

Q: Which is safer — 1xBet or Betway?

Both are trustworthy for African bettors. Betway holds more direct local licences (BCLB, NBS, NGB, GBT, GCA) making it more directly regulated in your country. 1xBet holds Curaçao licence plus local licences in some markets.

Q: Which has better welcome bonus — 1xBet or Betway?

1xBet typically offers a higher percentage bonus (up to 100-130% of first deposit) while Betway's bonus is 100% up to a smaller cap. 1xBet wins on bonus size; Betway wins on lower wagering requirements.

Q: Can I use OPay to deposit at Betway Nigeria?

No. As of 2026, Betway Nigeria does not accept OPay directly. Use GTBank, First Bank, or MTN MoMo for Betway Nigeria deposits. OPay is accepted at 1xBet Nigeria.
