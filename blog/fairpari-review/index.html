Is FairPari Legit and Safe?

FairPari is operated by an identifiable company, Centrald B.V., and the Curaçao Gaming Control Board's public register confirms fairpari.com as an approved domain under licence OGL/2024/1143/0865, granted 3 September 2024 and currently listed as active. That's a genuine, verifiable licence, not a hollow claim.

What it isn't is local licensing. A Curaçao gaming licence is not the same as being regulated by Nigeria's NLRC, Kenya's BCLB or Ghana's GCA, and it doesn't automatically mean online gambling is permitted where you live. Some African jurisdictions require locally licensed operators for legal online betting; others take a more permissive approach to offshore-licensed sites. Check your own country's rules rather than assuming a Curaçao licence settles the question either way.
