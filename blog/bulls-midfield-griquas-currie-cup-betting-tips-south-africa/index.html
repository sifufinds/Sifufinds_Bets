Licensing and regulation in South Africa

South African betting is overseen by three provincial gambling boards: the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB), and the Eastern Cape Gambling and Racing Board (ECGBB). These bodies enforce strict licensing standards, player protection measures and responsible gambling initiatives. Bookmakers such as Betway operate under a WCGRB licence, while FairPari is NGB licensed. Operators holding a Curaçao licence, like Linebet, are still permitted to accept South African players but are not directly regulated by the local boards. When choosing a platform, always check the licence information displayed on the site’s footer to ensure you are betting with a legally authorised operator.
