Licensing and regulation – why the Gaming Board of Tanzania matters

All the operators listed operate under a licence issued by the Gaming Board of Tanzania. This regulator enforces strict standards on player protection, fair play and financial transparency. A GBT‑licensed bookmaker must keep player funds in a separate account, undergo regular audits and provide clear terms for bonuses and withdrawals. By betting with a licensed provider you reduce the risk of fraud and gain access to a formal dispute‑resolution process should any issue arise.
