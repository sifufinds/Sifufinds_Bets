Licensing and Regulation in South Africa

South Africa’s gambling sector is overseen by the Western Cape Gambling Regulatory Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB), and provincial boards such as ECGBB. These bodies ensure that bookmakers operate fairly, provide responsible gambling tools, and protect player funds. Always check that your chosen bookmaker lists an NGB or WCGRB licence before depositing.
