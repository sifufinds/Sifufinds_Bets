Licensing and regulation in Tanzania

All betting activity in Tanzania is overseen by the Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT). The board issues licences to operators that meet strict criteria on financial stability, data protection and responsible gambling. SportPesa, 22Bet, 1xBet, FairPari and Betway all hold GBT licences, meaning they must adhere to local consumer‑protection standards and are subject to regular audits. By contrast, Linebet operates under a Curaçao licence, which does not fall under GBT supervision. While Curaçao‑licensed sites are common in Africa, they do not benefit from the same level of local oversight.
