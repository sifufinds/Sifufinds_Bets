WC2026 Group Stage — African Team Expert Picks

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is now live. The biggest tournament in football history — 48 teams, 16 groups, 104 matches — has kicked off across the USA, Canada, and Mexico. Africa has 9 teams at this World Cup, the most in history.

Here are our group stage betting tips for every African nation, with odds from top African bookmakers.

Morocco 🇲🇦 — Atlas Lions

Expert pick: Advance from group stage @1.18 (1xBet)

Morocco are Africa's best team and the standout African bet at WC2026. Their 2022 run to the semi-finals proved they belong at the highest level. Coach Walid Regragui has built a tactically disciplined side with world-class defensive solidity and direct, dangerous attacking play. Hakim Ziyech, Youssef En-Nesyri, and Achraf Hakimi give them the quality to beat any group-stage opponent.

Value markets: Morocco to qualify from group (near certainty), Morocco to reach QF (@3.50), En-Nesyri top African scorer.

Senegal 🇸🇳 — Lions of Teranga

Expert pick: Advance from group stage @1.28 (Betway)

AFCON champions Senegal arrive at WC2026 as Africa's second-strongest side. Sadio Mané, Idrissa Gueye, and Ismaila Sarr form a world-class core. Senegal's strength is their balance — they score freely and concede rarely. Their group stage advance should be near-certain against most opponents.

Value markets: Senegal to top group (@2.80 if in a soft group), Mané to score anytime (@2.20), Senegal 1+ goals in all 3 group games.

Cameroon 🇨🇲 — Indomitable Lions

Expert pick: First match BTTS @1.75 (Melbet)

Cameroon have a strong but inconsistent squad. Andre-Frank Zambo Anguissa is world-class at the base of midfield, and Karl Toko Ekambi is clinical in front of goal. Their weakness is concentration — they can concede set-piece goals in waves. BTTS is historically their best market.

DR Congo 🇨🇩 — Les Léopards

Expert pick: First match BTTS @1.90 (1xBet)

DR Congo make their first World Cup appearance in decades and bring genuine attacking talent from the European leagues. They'll concede but they'll also create chances. Don't expect them to advance from the group, but BTTS in their first match is genuine value.

---

Tunisia 🇹🇳 — Eagles of Carthage

Expert pick: Under 2.5 goals first match @1.70 (Betway)

Tunisia are Africa's most defensively organised side. They concede rarely and play compact, counter-attacking football. Low-scoring is their pattern — they drew 0-0 with France at WC2022. Under 2.5 goals first match is their most reliable market.

South Africa 🇿🇦 — Bafana Bafana

Expert pick: First match 1+ goals scored @1.45 (Hollywoodbets)

Bafana Bafana make their first World Cup since 2010 — an emotional, historic occasion. Expect high energy and a vocal continent behind them. They have quality in attack with Percy Tau still influential. South Africa scoring in their first match is excellent value.
