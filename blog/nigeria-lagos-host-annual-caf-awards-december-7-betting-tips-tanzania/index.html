Licensing and Regulation in Tanzania

All the bookmakers mentioned operate under a licence issued by the Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT). The board monitors operators for fair play, data protection and responsible gambling compliance. A GBT licence means the bookmaker must keep player funds in a segregated account and submit regular audit reports. Tanzanian bettors can trust that any dispute will be handled by the regulator, provided the operator displays the GBT badge on its website.

Payment Methods for Tanzanian Bettors

Mobile money dominates the Tanzanian betting landscape. M‑Pesa (Vodacom) and Airtel Money together account for the majority of deposits and withdrawals. Tigo Pesa and Halotel Pesa are growing, especially in regions where those networks have stronger coverage. Bank Transfer remains an option for larger deposits or for players who prefer a traditional banking route. Visa and Mastercard are accepted by 22Bet, 1xBet, FairPari and Betway, giving an alternative for those who already have card accounts. All transactions are processed in Tanzanian shillings (TZS) except FairPari, which converts the bonus into euros; the conversion rate is applied at the time of credit.

Responsible Gambling

Betting on the CAF Awards should be treated as entertainment, not a way to guarantee income. Set a budget before you start and never chase losses. The Gaming Board of Tanzania requires each licensed operator to provide self‑exclusion tools, deposit limits and a dedicated help line. If you feel your betting is becoming a problem, contact the board’s responsible gambling unit or use the bookmaker’s “Take a Break” feature. Remember, the excitement of the awards night is short‑lived; keep your gambling habits in check.
