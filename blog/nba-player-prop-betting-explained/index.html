What Are Player Props?

Before we get into the nitty-gritty, let’s define what player props are. In basketball betting, player props are wagers on specific individual performances during a game. This could be points scored, rebounds grabbed, assists dished out, or even something as quirky as whether a player will dye their hair (looking at you, Odell Beckham). These bets are a favorite among African punters because they let you focus on one player’s performance rather than the entire game’s outcome.
