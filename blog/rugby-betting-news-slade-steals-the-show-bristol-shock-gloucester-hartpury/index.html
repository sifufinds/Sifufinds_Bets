Betting on Rugby: First Try Scorer and Correct Score Markets

For those looking to bet on rugby, two popular markets are First Try Scorer and Correct Score. First Try Scorer involves betting on the player who will score the first try in a match, while Correct Score involves betting on the final score of the match.

In the First Try Scorer market, bettors can find odds on individual players to score the first try. For example, Henry Slade's odds to score the first try in the next match range from 2.50 to 4.50 at Bet9ja. Meanwhile, Sportybet offers a more enticing 5.00 for Slade to be the first try scorer.
