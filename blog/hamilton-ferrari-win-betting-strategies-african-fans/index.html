In-Race Betting Strategies

African fans can take advantage of in-race betting markets to cash in on the action. Here are a few strategies to consider:

  1. Safety Car Betting: Safety cars can have a significant impact on the outcome of the race. If you're confident that a safety car will be deployed, you can bet on it to happen. Bet9ja offers odds of 2.50 for a safety car to occur, while 1xBet has it at 2.80.
  2. Fastest Lap: The fastest lap market is another in-race betting opportunity. Hamilton, being the dominant force he was in the Barcelona-Catalunya GP, is a strong contender for this market. Betway has him at 2.20 to set the fastest lap, while Hollywoodbets has him at 2.30.
  3. Driver to Be Fastest in a Segment: This market allows you to bet on a driver to be the fastest in a specific segment of the race. For example, you can bet on Hamilton to be the fastest in the first 10 laps. Betika offers odds of 2.80 for this market, while Melbet has it at 3.00.

Impact of Safety Cars on F1 Markets

Safety cars can significantly impact F1 markets, particularly those related to the fastest lap and driver to be fastest in a segment. When a safety car is deployed, the fastest lap market is suspended, and the winner of the restarted race is awarded the fastest lap.

To mitigate this risk, African fans can consider hedging their bets. For example, if you've bet on Hamilton to set the fastest lap, you can hedge your bet by placing a smaller wager on another driver to set the fastest lap if a safety car is deployed.
