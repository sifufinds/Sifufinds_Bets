<h3 id="how-does-withholding-tax-affect-my-betting-returns">How does withholding tax affect my betting returns?</h3>

<p>In regulated environments, platforms may apply a withholding tax (such as 10%) directly on your gross winnings at the moment of withdrawal. This means the tax is calculated on the total amount returned to you, not just the profit generated from the stake, fundamentally reducing your overall yield.</p>

<h3 id="why-do-platforms-enforce-daily-withdrawal-limits">Why do platforms enforce daily withdrawal limits?</h3>

<p>Operators use daily withdrawal caps, such as GHS 30,000 or GHS 50,000, to manage their own daily liquidity and mitigate the risk of fraud. It prevents a massive, sudden drain of capital from their ledgers following a highly predictable weekend of sports results.</p>

<h3 id="do-i-have-to-use-a-specific-mobile-network-to-deposit">Do I have to use a specific mobile network to deposit?</h3>

<p>While you can often use secondary payment gateways, platforms with direct telecom integrations offer significantly faster processing if you use their preferred networks. Using an unsupported network usually forces your transaction through slower third-party aggregators.</p>
