Licensing and Regulation in Ethiopia

Betting in Ethiopia falls under the oversight of the National Lottery Administration (NLA). The regulator ensures that all operators with a physical presence in the country adhere to strict anti‑money‑laundering (AML) and responsible‑gaming standards. While the bookmakers listed above operate under international licences, they still must register with the NLA to offer services to Ethiopian residents. This dual‑licence model protects players by combining global best practices with local oversight.

Payment Methods for Ethiopian Bettors
