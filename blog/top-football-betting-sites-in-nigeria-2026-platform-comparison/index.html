FAQ

Why is local licensing important for sports betting?

Local licensing ensures that the bookmaker is legally bound to honour payouts and secure your data according to regional laws, providing a legal recourse if funds are unfairly withheld.

Do independent comparison platforms process bets?

No, independent comparison platforms aggregate odds and verify operators. You must register and deposit directly with the licensed third-party bookmaker to place a wager.

What happens if I exceed the maximum payout limit on a winning bet?

The bookmaker will only pay out up to their stated maximum limit, regardless of what the original odds multiplied by your stake calculated.

Can I use Nigerian mobile money on international platforms?

It depends entirely on the operator's regional integration. While local bookmakers natively support MTN MoMo and OPay, international sites may require standard bank transfers or cards.
