How to Deposit with M-Pesa at Kenyan Betting Sites

M-Pesa is the payment method of choice at all BCLB-licensed Kenyan bookmakers. Here's how to deposit:

  1. Dial \*334# on your Safaricom line
  2. Select "Lipa na M-Pesa" → "Pay Bill"
  3. Enter the bookmaker's Paybill number
  4. Enter your betting account number as Account Number
  5. Enter deposit amount (minimum KSh 5–10 depending on site)
  6. Enter your M-Pesa PIN and confirm

Funds appear in your betting account within seconds.

Kenya Betting Regulation — BCLB

All licensed Kenyan bookmakers operate under the Betting Control and Licensing Board (BCLB), established under the Betting, Lotteries and Gaming Act. Kenyan bettors should only use BCLB-licensed sites — you can verify any operator's licence on the BCLB official website.

Kenya's betting tax is 7.5% excise duty on stakes (paid by the bettor). This means if you deposit KSh 100, KSh 7.50 goes to tax and KSh 92.50 is credited to your betting account. Factor this into your staking plans.
