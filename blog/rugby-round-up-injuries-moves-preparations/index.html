Rugby Betting Guide for African Fans

Rugby is a major betting sport in South Africa (Springboks), Kenya (Sevens), and Zimbabwe. The URC, Super Rugby, and international Tests attract significant betting volumes.

  • Match winner — 80-minute result or including draw
