Aston Villa keen on loan deal for Real Madrid wonderkid Endrick, while Manchester United eye Leipzig left back David Raum. The summer transfer window is heating up in Africa, with top clubs vying for the best talent.

In a recent development, Aston Villa are reportedly interested in signing Real Madrid's 18-year-old sensation Endrick on loan (Sky Sports). This move would be a significant boost to Villa's attack, given Endrick's impressive skills and goal-scoring record. As per Sky Sports, Villa have been tracking the young Brazilian for some time and believe he has the potential to become a key player in their squad.

Meanwhile, Manchester United are eyeing Leipzig left back David Raum (ESPN). The 26-year-old German international has impressed with his pace, skill, and defensive solidity, making him an attractive signing for United. With Raum's acquisition, Ole Gunnar Solskjær will have more options to choose from at the back.

Nottingham Forest have made a significant signing in Ousmane Diomande, who joins from Sporting (bbc.com). The 22-year-old Ivorian midfielder is expected to provide energy and creativity to Steve Cooper's team. This move could potentially boost Forest's chances of avoiding relegation this season.

Chelsea transfer news suggests that Mykhailo Mudryk is expected to leave the club on loan (bbc.com). The Ukrainian winger has struggled to adapt to the Premier League, and a temporary exit could help him regain his form and confidence. This development might affect Chelsea's odds for winning the league or finishing in the top four.

Chelsea news also links Enzo Fernandez with a potential move to Manchester City (BBC). While there's no confirmation on whether this deal will happen, it's clear that both clubs are keen on acquiring the young Argentine midfielder.

Manchester United transfer rumors continue to swirl around David Raum, with Sky Sports and BBC providing updates on his potential signing. Given his impressive performances for Leipzig, it's no surprise that United are interested in bringing him to Old Trafford.
