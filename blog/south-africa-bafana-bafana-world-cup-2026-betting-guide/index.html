South Africa's WC2026 Qualification Story

Bafana Bafana's return to the World Cup ended a 20‑year absence since their last appearance at the 2010 FIFA World Cup hosted on home soil. The qualification campaign under Hugo Broos was built on defensive organization and the brilliance of goalkeeper Ronwen Williams, who earned a FIFA ranking as the world's best goalkeeper in 2024.

Key qualifier performances included a famous 2‑0 victory over Morocco in Johannesburg, a result that shifted the group hierarchy and showed that Bafana can compete with Africa's elite.

Coach Hugo Broos: A Comparative Lens

Broos, a Dutch former player, previously coached the national teams of Nigeria and Ghana. Under his guidance, Nigeria reached the 2014 African Nations Championship final, while Ghana finished third in the 2015 Africa Cup of Nations. His emphasis on tactical discipline and physical conditioning translates well to the rigour of the World Cup stage.
