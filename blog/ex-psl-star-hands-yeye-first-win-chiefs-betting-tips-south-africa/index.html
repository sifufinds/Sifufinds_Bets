Licensing and regulation in South Africa

Betting in South Africa is overseen by a network of provincial gambling boards – the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). These bodies enforce strict compliance with responsible gambling standards, anti‑money‑laundering measures and fair‑play rules. Operators that carry a WCGRB or NGB licence, such as Betway and FairPari, are subject to regular audits and must display their licence numbers on their websites. International licences (Curaçao, other jurisdictions) are also permitted, but players should verify that the operator adheres to South African consumer‑protection legislation before depositing large sums.
