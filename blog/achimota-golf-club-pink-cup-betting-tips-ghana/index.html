Licensing and regulation in Ghana

All the bookmakers highlighted above operate under a licence issued by the Gaming Commission of Ghana (GCA). The GCA is the statutory body responsible for protecting players, ensuring fair play and preventing money‑laundering. A GCA licence means the operator must:

  • Keep player funds in a segregated account separate from operating capital.
  • Provide transparent terms for bonuses, wagering and withdrawals.
  • Submit regular audits to the Commission.

Because the GCA enforces strict age verification, you must be at least 18 years old to open an account. Any breach of the T&Cs can result in account suspension or loss of bonus eligibility. Always read the full terms on the bookmaker’s site before you deposit.
