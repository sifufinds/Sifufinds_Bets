Licensing and regulation in Tanzania

All the bookmakers listed except Linebet operate under a licence issued by the Gaming Board of Tanzania. The GBT regulates licensing, ensures fair play, monitors anti‑money‑laundering compliance and enforces responsible‑gambling standards. Operators must submit regular audit reports and are subject to random inspections. When you see “GBT Licensed” on a site, you can be confident that the platform adheres to Tanzanian law and that your funds are protected by local oversight.

Payment methods popular among Tanzanian punters

Tanzania’s mobile‑money ecosystem is the backbone of online betting deposits. M‑Pay (Vodacom) and Airtel Money dominate because they allow instant transfers directly from a phone’s balance. Choose the method that offers the lowest transaction fee and the quickest credit time – usually M‑Pay for deposits under TSh 10,000 and Bank Transfer for larger top‑ups.
