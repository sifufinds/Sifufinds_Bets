AFCON 2027 Qualification: Group Stage Analysis

The Africa Cup of Nations 2027, co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania, is still a year away — but the qualification drama is already heating up. Here's your guide to the top betting markets.

Group A Standouts: Nigeria & Ghana

The Super Eagles and the Black Stars are both expected to qualify comfortably, but their direct clash in matchday 4 is one of the most anticipated fixtures in African football. Nigeria are short favourites at 1.85 to win, with Ghana at 4.20 and the draw at 3.40.

Value bet: Ghana +1.5 Asian Handicap at 1.60 — The Black Stars rarely lose by 2+ against top AFCON nations.

Group C Dark Horse: Senegal

The defending champions are navigating a difficult group after a poor run of form following AFCON 2025. Sadio Mané has retired from international duty, leaving a creative vacuum. However, their defensive solidity remains elite.

Value bet: Senegal to qualify (head-to-head) at 1.45 — they've never failed to qualify in the last decade.

AFCON 2027 Qualification Groups

The road to AFCON 2027 began in late 2025 with 54 CAF member nations split into 12 qualifying groups of 4-5 teams. The top two from each group, plus the best third-placed teams, advance to the tournament co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.

Group Stage Betting Markets

Outright group winners are the most popular AFCON qualifier market. Value often lies in:

  • Second-placed qualification (often overlooked at bookmakers)
  • Goal scorer markets for star players in qualifying matches
  • Home/away match result for specific fixtures

Top Teams in AFCON 2027 Qualifying

Morocco — Dominant in Qualification

Morocco are expected to cruise through their qualifying group. The Atlas Lions lost their first AFCON qualifying match in years in 2024 but reasserted dominance quickly. Odds to qualify for AFCON 2027: @1.05 (effectively certainty).

Nigeria — Expected to Qualify

Nigeria Super Eagles have qualified for every AFCON since 2004. Despite coaching changes, their squad depth ensures qualification in most scenarios. Watch for Osimhen in qualifying matches — he typically scores freely against weaker opposition.

Ghana — Rebuilding Under New Coach

Ghana's qualification is less certain following their 2023 AFCON group stage exit. Their new coach faces the challenge of rebuilding defensive solidity while maintaining Black Stars' attacking talent. Value at @1.40 to qualify from their group.

Ivory Coast — Defending Champions

Les Éléphants as AFCON 2023 champions face higher expectations. They should qualify comfortably from most groups. Back them as group winners at value odds.

AFCON 2027 Qualifying — Best Betting Strategies

  1. Back strong nations early — Morocco, Nigeria, Senegal qualifying prices tighten as qualification progresses. Best value is before the campaign starts.
  2. Track injury news — AFCON qualifiers are often affected by players prioritising club football over international duty. Monitor squad selection announcements.
  3. Home advantage matters — Home teams in African qualifying have a significantly higher win rate than in European qualification due to travel difficulties, extreme heat, and partisan crowds.
  4. Upset value — Every qualifying campaign produces surprises. Look for home rank outsiders with strong local form facing tired travelling sides.
