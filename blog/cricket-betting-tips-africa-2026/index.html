IPL vs International Cricket — Different Betting Approaches

IPL (T20): High-scoring, fast-paced. Sixes markets, team totals, and powerplay runs are popular. High variance in results.

Test Cricket: Longer, more analytical. Draw is a genuine outcome. Session betting and wicket markets offer value for knowledgeable bettors.

T20 Internationals: Balance of both — team totals and match winner are the most popular markets.
