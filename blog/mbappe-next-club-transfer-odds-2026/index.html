Mbappé Set for Another Summer Soap Opera

For the second consecutive summer, Kylian Mbappé's future is the biggest story in world football. Despite signing for Real Madrid, reports from Marca and L'Équipe suggest the Frenchman is unsettled under Ancelotti and could seek a move.

The Leading Destinations

PSG Return (7.50) – A sensational homecoming to Paris is never fully off the table. Al-Khelaïfi reportedly maintains contact. Odds have drifted since reports of squad unrest, but still attracting money.

Premier League Move — Arsenal (12.00) – The Gunners' project is the most exciting in England. Arteta has already spoken publicly about wanting to add another elite attacker. At 12.00, there's speculative value.

Al-Hilal Saudi Arabia (4.50) – The Saudi Pro League project remains aggressive. The money is generational. Mbappé turns 28 in December and could still return to Europe at 30 after a lucrative stint.

Stay at Real Madrid (1.85) – Ancelotti has publicly backed his star man. The short price reflects the most likely outcome — but it's a volatile market.
