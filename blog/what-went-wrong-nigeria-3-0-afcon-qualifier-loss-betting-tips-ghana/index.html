Key Factors That Cost Nigeria

Ghana’s midfield trio dictated the tempo, limiting Nigeria’s ability to build attacks. The Ghanaian players were able to win duels and retain possession, leaving Nigeria’s forwards exposed.

  1. Defensive Lapses

Nigeria’s back line suffered from a lack of coordination. Two critical errors allowed Ghana to score twice in quick succession, and the final goal came from a rebound after a defensive miscommunication.

  1. Psychological Pressure

The weight of expectation on Nigeria’s squad was immense. Players appeared tense and hesitant, which translated into fewer shots on target and a slower decision‑making process.

  1. Tactical Inflexibility

The coach’s strategy did not adapt to Ghana’s aggressive pressing. While Ghana pressed high, Nigeria’s formation remained too rigid, making it difficult to switch to a more defensive posture when needed.
