The Thomas Partey Visa Saga

Ghana’s midfield linchpin Thomas Partey saw his Canadian visa appeal rejected, meaning he will miss the opening match against Panama. Partey’s absence removes a key ball‑winning element from Ghana’s setup – he averages 2.3 interceptions and 1.9 key passes per game in the qualifiers. Without him, Ghana will likely shift to a more direct approach, leaning on Mohammed Kudus and the pace of Jordan Ayew.

From a betting angle, Ghana’s odds have lengthened across the board. At Hollywoodbets, Ghana to win against Panama is now 3.75 ZAR, up from 2.90 ZAR before the news broke. Conversely, the pan‑first‑goal market has shortened, with Panama at 1.95 ZAR. Bettors should watch the total corners market; Ghana traditionally win many set‑pieces, and a reduction in midfield creativity could see the total corners drop below 9.5, currently priced at 1.90 ZAR on Bet9ja.
