Real Madrid News: Pérez Re-elected, Mourinho Confirmed

Florentino Pérez’s re‑election as Real Madrid president was a landslide victory, with 98% of the vote cast in his favour. The 57‑year‑old chairman has overseen the club’s most successful era in the last decade, steering a squad that has won 12 La Liga titles and five Champions League trophies since 2012. His return signals a commitment to continuity, especially in the transfer market where the club’s “Galácticos” strategy has shifted towards a blend of marquee signings and young talent.

José Mourinho’s Arrival

The appointment of José Mourinho as head coach marks a new chapter. Mourinho’s résumé includes five league titles across England, Spain, Italy, and Portugal, and two Champions League trophies with Porto and Inter Milan. His tactical acumen—particularly his emphasis on defensive solidity and set‑piece threat—could prove invaluable in a La Liga that is currently tight between Real Madrid, Barcelona, and Atlético Madrid.

Croatia and Modrić: A Farewell Performance?

Luka Modrić’s goal against Slovenia in a friendly was a fitting farewell on home soil. The 35‑year‑old midfielder, who has won the Ballon d’Or in 2018, announced his intention to retire from international duty after the 2026 World Cup. Modrić’s influence on Croatian football is immeasurable: he has won 16 caps with 12 goals, and his leadership helped Croatia reach the World Cup final in 2018.

Impact on the National Team

Croatia’s midfield now faces a void. Their current squad depth includes players like Mateo Kovačić and Franck Kessie, but none match Modrić’s vision and passing range. Eriksen Collapse: A Concerning Incident

Christian Eriksen’s collapse during a friendly between Denmark and Ukraine shocked fans worldwide. The 33‑year‑old midfielder was taken off the pitch and received immediate medical attention. He was later declared conscious and is currently receiving treatment in a Danish hospital.

Medical Context

Eriksen’s case highlights the importance of on‑field medical protocols. According to FIFA’s Medical Committee, rapid response teams must be present at all professional matches, equipped with automated external defibrillators (AEDs) and trained personnel. In Africa, the availability of such equipment varies widely: in Ghana, the national stadiums are now mandated to have AEDs, but many local clubs still lack basic first‑aid kits.

Betting Implications

While this incident is primarily a health concern, it also affects betting markets. For instance, the “next player to score a goal” market for Denmark may shift if Eriksen’s fitness is uncertain. AFC News: Algeria's Enigma

Algeria’s performance at the 2026 World Cup qualification was a study in paradoxes. After a comfortable qualifying run, the national team exited the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) at the group stage, leaving fans perplexed. Analysts point to a combination of tactical inconsistencies and a lack of depth in the squad.

Tactical Overview

Algeria’s coach, Rudi Garcia, prefers a 4‑3‑3 formation but struggles to adapt when key players are injured. Their average possession in the AFCON was 47%, compared to 57% for their top rivals. The team’s defensive record was also shaky, conceding an average of 1.3 goals per game.

Feyenoord Sack Van Persie: What’s Next?

Feyenoord’s decision to sack Robin van Persie after one season shocked Dutch football. Van Persie, a former England striker, was tasked with reviving a club that had not won the Eredivisie since 2017. The club’s performance under his stewardship was underwhelming: a 15th‑place finish and a lack of goals.

Managerial Turnover

The Dutch league has a high rate of managerial turnover, averaging 3.2 managers per season. Feyenoord’s decision reflects the club’s ambition to climb back to European qualification.
