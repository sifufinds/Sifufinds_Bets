Betting Tips for the Series

When betting on the Kenya NCBA Golf Series, focus on markets that offer the best value.

  1. Match Winner – The most straightforward market, and the one most bookmakers offer the best odds for. Look for underdogs with strong recent form; a small stake can turn into a sizeable return if the underdog pulls off an upset.
  2. Top Three Finishers – This market pays well if you pick a golfer who is not the favourite but has a realistic chance of finishing in the top three.
  3. Exact Score – A riskier option but can pay out heavily if you predict the exact final score or the score after a particular round.
  4. Over/Under Total Strokes – If you understand the course layout, this can be a profitable way to bet on the total number of strokes taken by a player.

Always read the conditions of each market; some bookmakers require a minimum odds threshold or a minimum stake for certain bets.
