Rugby Betting Guide for African Fans

Rugby is a major betting sport in South Africa, Kenya, and Zimbabwe, with the United Rugby Championship (URC), Super Rugby, and international Tests attracting significant betting volumes. Here’s a breakdown of the most popular rugby markets:

  • Match Winner: Bet on the team to win the match (80 minutes or including a draw).
  • Match Handicap: Popular for mismatched games, where one team is given a points advantage.
  • Total Points: Over/under bets on the combined score of both teams.
  • First Try Scorer: High-odds market for predicting the player to score the first try.
  • Half-Time/Full-Time: Combined result for both halves of the match.
