This article covers what has actually been confirmed, what is still being reported, and what remains unconfirmed across Nigeria's NPFL, Kenya's FKF Premier League (KPL), Ghana's GPL and South Africa's DStv Premiership (PSL), plus three closely-watched moves involving African internationals in Europe.

Nigeria: NPFL Clubs Rebuild Before the 28 August Kick-Off

The 2026/27 Nigeria Premier Football League season is scheduled to start on 28 and 30 August 2026, and clubs have been busy in the weeks before kick-off. Reigning champions Rangers International confirmed on 13 August 2026 that midfielder Ifeanyi Okechukwu had joined from Nasarawa United on a contract running to 2028, giving manager Fidelis Ilechukwu extra midfield depth as Rangers defend their title.

Elsewhere in the NPFL, Shooting Stars have been among the most active clubs, bringing in midfielder Olakunle Akala from Kano Pillars, Abdulaziz Dalhatu from Niger Tornadoes and Taheer Abubakar from Wikki Tourists. Katsina United have made a dozen additions, including players from Rivers United, Remo Stars and Akwa United, plus goalkeeper Koroma Francis, a Sierra Leone recruit. Kano Pillars, Niger Tornadoes and Ranchers Bees have also confirmed new signings ahead of the campaign.

Kenya: Gor Mahia's Overhaul Dominates the KPL Window

Kenya's top-flight transfer window opened on 21 June 2026 and is scheduled to close on 21 August 2026. Gor Mahia have made the biggest splash, confirming nine new arrivals including Daniel Sakari, a Harambee Stars right-back who joined from Kenya Police, along with Paul Okoth, Michael Onyango, David Okoth, Lesley Otieno, Hansel Ochieng, Humphrey Katasi, Enock Machaka and Ebenezer Ocran, according to Pulse Sports Kenya.

AFC Leopards have confirmed four arrivals of their own, headlined by Ugandan winger Taddeo Lwanga from Vipers SC, alongside Robinson Musungu, Eric Balecho and Sebastian Wekesa. Tusker FC have added eight players including Nigerian striker Victor Mbaoma from Remo Stars, while Nairobi United, KCB and Shabana have also completed multiple signings ahead of the new season.

Ghana: Amankona Comes Home to Aduana FC

Aduana FC have confirmed the signing of striker Stephen Amankona on a three-year deal, according to GHANAsoccernet. The 26-year-old returns to Ghana after a spell with Egyptian side Smouha SC, having previously won back-to-back GPL Golden Boot awards in the 2023/24 and 2024/25 seasons while at Berekum Chelsea. It is one of the more significant confirmed moves ahead of Ghana's 2026/27 top-flight campaign.

South Africa: Orlando Pirates Reshape Their Squad

Fresh from winning their fifth South African Premiership title in May 2026, Orlando Pirates have reshaped their squad for the DStv Premiership's 2026/27 season. In a swap deal confirmed in early June 2026, the Buccaneers signed Stellenbosch FC midfielder Mthetheleli Mthiyane on a three-year contract, with defender Thabiso Lebitso moving to Stellenbosch the other way, according to SABC Sport. Separately, Pirates and Durban City agreed the permanent transfer of 22-year-old attacking midfielder Seth Green, confirmed on 6 July 2026, ending his time in the Pirates academy setup without a first-team appearance for the Soweto giants.

African Talent on the Move in Europe

Three deals involving African internationals have dominated headlines in the European market this month.

Confirmed: Nottingham Forest completed the signing of Ivory Coast defender Ousmane Diomande from Sporting CP on 11 August 2026, in a deal reported at around 40 million euros (£34.3m) on a five-year contract, according to Sky Sports. Diomande was part of Ivory Coast's Africa Cup of Nations-winning squad in 2024 and Forest saw off interest from Arsenal and Manchester United to sign him.

Confirmed: Coventry City, back in the Premier League for the first time since 2001 after winning last season's Championship, completed a club-record signing of Ghana midfielder Caleb Yirenkyi from Danish side Nordsjaelland on 7 August 2026. ESPN reported the fee at around 27 million euros (£23.1m), with a further 3 million euros (£2.5m) available in add-ons, following Yirenkyi's performances for Ghana at this year's World Cup.

Reported, not confirmed: Nigeria striker Victor Osimhen has been strongly linked with a move from Galatasaray to Arsenal, with Sky Sports and other outlets reporting that the two clubs have opened talks. As of 14 August 2026, Galatasaray have not set an asking price and no fee or contract has been agreed, so this remains a reported negotiation rather than a confirmed transfer. SifuFinds will update this article if and when a deal is officially completed.

Key Takeaways

  • Nigeria's NPFL season starts 28-30 August 2026; Rangers have already reinforced their title defence with Ifeanyi Okechukwu.
  • Kenya's KPL transfer window closes 21 August 2026; Gor Mahia have made nine confirmed signings, the most of any club.
  • Ghana's Aduana FC have confirmed a three-year deal for two-time GPL Golden Boot winner Stephen Amankona.

FAQ

The Nigeria Premier Football League's new season is scheduled to kick off on 28 and 30 August 2026, according to Nigerian football outlets covering the pre-season transfer window.

Q: Which African player has completed the biggest confirmed transfer this window?
