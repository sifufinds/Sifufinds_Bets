Tanzania Betting Regulation — GBT

All licensed bookmakers in Tanzania operate under the Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT). Bettors should only use GBT-licensed operators. Tanzania has one of East Africa's most mature regulatory frameworks, with the GBT requiring operators to maintain local customer support and process payments within 48 hours.

How to Deposit at Tanzanian Betting Sites

M-Pesa Tanzania (Vodacom)

  1. Dial *150*00# on your Vodacom Tanzania line
  2. Select "Lipa Bili" (Pay Bill)
  3. Enter the bookmaker's Vodacom Business number
  4. Enter your betting account number
  5. Enter amount (minimum TSh 1,000) and confirm with PIN

Tigo Pesa

  1. Dial *150*01# → "Malipo" → "Lipa Bili"
  2. Enter the bookmaker's Tigo business number and your account number

Airtel Money Tanzania

  1. Dial *150*60# → "Malipo" → "Lipa Bili"

All three platforms process deposits instantly. Withdrawals typically take 5–30 minutes.
