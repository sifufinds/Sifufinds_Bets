FAQ: iGaming in Africa

1. What works in Kenya can fail in Nigeria—why?

Local payment options, language settings, and regulatory environments vary widely across African countries. For example, M-Pesa is a dominant payment method in Kenya, while MTN MoMo is more popular in Nigeria. Bookmakers must tailor their offerings to each market’s unique needs.

2. How do global regulatory changes affect African bettors?

Global trends, like increased taxation or stricter regulations, often trickle down to African markets. Operators may pass on higher costs to bettors through reduced odds or fewer promotions. Staying informed and comparing bookmakers is key.
