Just 6 minutes ago, Talking Rugby Union reported that Ben Spencer will miss the start of the PREM Season. The Bath captain, who recently underwent surgery, is a massive blow for the club, as per BBC Rugby. This is the kind of squad news that typically moves match odds and outright markets. With Spencer out, Bath’s leadership and scrum-half options are thinner, which could affect their performance against top teams.

Springboks Bench Feinberg-Mngomezulu for All Blacks Rematch

In a bold move, the Springboks have dropped Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu to the bench for their rematch against the All Blacks, as reported by BBC Rugby. The fly-half’s indifferent performance in the series opener has cost him his starting spot. This tactical shift could impact the first try scorer and correct score markets, as South Africa’s attacking strategy may change.

First try scorer markets are always popular among African bettors, especially on platforms like Bet9ja and Hollywoodbets. With Feinberg-Mngomezulu on the bench, the Boks might rely more on their forwards for early tries. Keep an eye on players like Eben Etzebeth, who recently featured in FloRugby’s top 100 players list.

Off-Field Stories: Kyle Tayler-Woolston and the Kings Cross Steelers

Rugby isn’t just about what happens on the pitch. Kyle Tayler-Woolston’s story, as reported by BBC Rugby, shows how the sport is becoming more inclusive. The gay rugby player, who feared rejection, is now flourishing in Super Rygbi Cymru thanks to the support at Newport.

Meanwhile, the Kings Cross Steelers won the 2026 Bingham Cup, as per Star Observer. This victory highlights the growing acceptance of LGBTQ+ players in rugby. While these stories don’t directly impact betting markets, they remind us of rugby’s broader impact on society.

Richie Mo’unga’s All Blacks Return

In a surprising turn of events, Richie Mo’unga is back with the All Blacks, as reported by The Times. The fly-half’s return was inspired by watching the Six Nations in a bar. This addition strengthens New Zealand’s backline, which could affect their odds in upcoming matches.

For African bettors, this is a signal to monitor the All Blacks’ markets. Will Mo’unga’s return make them favorites in the Rugby Championship? Compare odds on Betika and BetKing to see how bookmakers are reacting.

Wales Push the Pain Barrier Ahead of WXV Global Series

Wales are putting in the hard yards ahead of the WXV Global Series, as per BBC Rugby. The team’s pre-season camp is intense, with players pushing their limits to improve. This kind of preparation often translates to better performances on the field, which could make Wales a dark horse in the tournament.

Betting Markets to Watch: First Try Scorer and Correct Score

Rugby betting offers a variety of markets, but first try scorer and correct score are two of the most exciting. Here’s how they work:

  • First Try Scorer: Bet on the player you think will score the first try of the match. This market requires research into team tactics and player form. For example, if the Springboks are relying on their forwards, betting on a prop or lock could be a smart move.
  • Correct Score: Predict the exact final score of the match. This market is high-risk, high-reward and requires a deep understanding of both teams’ strengths and weaknesses.

Both markets are available on Bet9ja, Hollywoodbets, and 1xBet. Always compare odds across platforms to ensure you’re getting the best value.
