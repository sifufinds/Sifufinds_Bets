The Nigeria Football Federation (NFF) has just released its registration guidelines for the 2027 President Federation Cup, setting a clear financial framework for clubs that want to compete. Men’s teams face a fee of ₦200,000, while women’s squads will pay ₦150,000, as announced by CompleteSports.com. These figures will shape the competitive landscape and, for bettors, create fresh wagering opportunities across the country.

Why the new fee matters to Nigerian bettors

------------------------------------------

The President Federation Cup is a cornerstone of Nigerian football, attracting clubs from the NPFL and lower divisions alike. With the 2027 edition now firmly on the calendar, the fee structure indicates the NFF’s commitment to a more professional and financially sustainable competition. For fans and punters, this translates into a growing pool of matches to bet on, ranging from group stages to the knockout rounds.

Betting markets to watch

------------------------

The Cup offers a wealth of traditional markets: match winner, both teams to score, first goal scorer, and over/under totals. Because of the knockout nature of the later stages, markets such as “team to win the cup” or “first team to score” become especially enticing.
