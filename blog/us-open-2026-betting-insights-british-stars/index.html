Arthur Fery’s Rise: A ‘Fery-tale’ Journey to the US Open

As reported by Guardian Tennis, British No. 2 Arthur Fery has been making waves after a challenging year marked by injury. Reflecting on his journey, Fery shared, “There was a lot of doubt and pain,” but his recent form suggests he’s ready for his US Open debut. After climbing from around 300 in the world, Fery’s resilience is a testament to his determination.

For bettors, Fery’s story is one to watch. Players coming off injury often have fluctuating odds, and his current form could offer value in the early rounds. Heather Watson Eyes US Open Return

According to BBC Tennis, Heather Watson is among the British players making strides in US Open qualifying. Watson, aiming for her first main-draw appearance in New York in five years, secured a first-round win. Her experience and recent form make her a player to monitor, especially in the qualifying stages where upsets are common.

Qualifying matches often present betting opportunities, as odds can be volatile. Keep an eye on 1xBet and Betway for live markets as Watson and other qualifiers battle for a spot in the main draw.

Stan Wawrinka’s Wild Card Snub Shocks Fans

In a surprising turn of events, New York Post reports that Stan Wawrinka was not granted a wild card for what could be his final Grand Slam appearance. This decision has left fans and bettors alike questioning the US Open’s strategy. Without Wawrinka in the mix, the men’s draw opens up for younger players like Daniil Medvedev, who’s preparing in Winston-Salem, as noted by Tennisuptodate.com.

Wawrinka’s absence could impact outright betting markets, potentially shortening odds for top seeds. Check Hollywoodbets and Melbet for updated prices on favorites like Medvedev, who’s looking sharp ahead of the tournament.

In-Play Tennis Betting: Strategies for African Punters

In-play betting is where tennis truly shines, offering dynamic markets that react to every point. Here are some strategies tailored for African bettors:

  1. Monitor Early-Round Upsets: Qualifiers and early rounds often see unexpected results. Players like Rebecca Marino, who advanced to the second round of qualifying as per Penticton Herald, can provide value if they carry their momentum forward.
  1. Watch for Momentum Shifts: Tennis matches can turn on a dime. If a player like Fery or Watson starts strong, their in-play odds will shorten quickly. Be ready to act fast—platforms like Betika and BetKing offer live markets that update in real-time.
  1. Focus on Set Betting: Set betting is a great way to capitalize on a player’s form within a match. For instance, if a player wins the first set convincingly, their odds to win the match will drop, but set betting can still offer decent returns.
  1. Track Player Fitness: Injury-prone players or those coming off long matches may struggle in later rounds. Keep an eye on reports from outlets like LTA and olympics.com for updates on player conditions.
