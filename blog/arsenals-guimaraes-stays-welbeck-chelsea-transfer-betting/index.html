FAQ

Q1: How often do transfer odds change after a medical?

Odds can shift dramatically within minutes of a medical confirmation. A “Yes” ticket that was 9.0 before the medical may drop to 4.5 after a successful test, while the “No” side rises accordingly.

Q2: Can I bet on a player’s destination before the club is announced?

Yes. Many bookmakers offer “player‑to‑club” markets (e.g., “Welbeck to Chelsea”). These are high‑volatility but can deliver big payouts if you read the rumors correctly.

Q3: Are there any special promotions for transfer markets?

African bookmakers frequently run “Free Bet” or “Enhanced Odds” promos on big transfer stories. Check the promotions tab on Bet9ja, Betway and 1xBet for limited‑time offers.

Q4: What should I do if a transfer falls through at the last minute?

Most reputable sportsbooks will void the bet if the transfer does not happen before the market close (usually midnight on deadline day). Keep an eye on the “void if not confirmed” clause in the bet slip.
