Two confirmed transfers at very different levels of English football.

Guimaraes → Arsenal (Completed, Despite Early Reports of a Stall)

Arsenal agreed a £75m fee with Newcastle for captain Bruno Guimaraes, with the deal reached on 5 August 2026 and completed by 9 August. The Brazilian signed a four-year contract with a club option for a further year. Earlier reports had suggested the move was stalling, with Newcastle standing firm on their valuation, but the deal ultimately went through.

Wrexham Sign Danny Imray

Wrexham completed the signing of Crystal Palace full-back Danny Imray for a reported £5m, their first incoming signing of the summer transfer window. The 23-year-old, who can also play right wing-back, signed a four-year contract.
