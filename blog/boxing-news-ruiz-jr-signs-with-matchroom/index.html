Common Boxing Terms To Know

There are several common boxing terms to know, including:

  • Knockout (KO): When a boxer is knocked down and cannot continue to fight.
  • Technical Knockout (TKO): When a boxer is injured and cannot continue to fight.
  • Points Decision: When the fight goes the full distance and the winner is decided by the judges' scorecards.
  • Draw: When the fight is tied and there is no winner.
