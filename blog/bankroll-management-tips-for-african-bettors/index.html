The Core Principles of Bankroll Management

1. Set Your Bankroll — And Never Exceed It

Your bankroll is the total amount you've set aside specifically for betting. It is money you can afford to lose without affecting your living expenses. Never use rent money, savings, or borrowed funds for betting.

Recommended starting bankrolls:

  • Casual bettor: ₦10,000–₦50,000 (Nigeria), KSh 2,000–KSh 10,000 (Kenya)
  • Serious bettor: 3× your average monthly disposable income
  • Maximum per-bet stake: 5% of your total bankroll

2. The Flat Staking Method

The simplest and most sustainable staking method: stake the same amount on every bet, regardless of confidence level.

Example: ₦50,000 bankroll → ₦2,500 flat stake per bet (5%)

This prevents the common mistake of chasing losses with larger stakes after a bad run.

3. The Kelly Criterion (For Advanced Bettors)

The Kelly Criterion calculates the optimal stake based on your perceived edge:

Kelly Stake % = (Odds × Probability - 1) ÷ (Odds - 1)

Example: You believe Morocco have a 55% chance of winning at odds of 2.00.

Kelly % = (2.00 × 0.55 - 1) ÷ (2.00 - 1) = (1.10 - 1) ÷ 1 = 10%

Most professionals use fractional Kelly (25–50% of the Kelly output) to reduce variance.

4. Tracking Your Bets

Keep a simple record of every bet:

  • Date, selection, odds, stake, result, profit/loss
  • Monthly review: what categories are profitable vs unprofitable?
  • Identify your edge (leagues, bet types, markets you're best at)

A Google Sheet or Excel file is sufficient. Apps like BetTracker or BetStats are also useful.

Biggest Bankroll Management Mistakes

  1. Chasing losses — doubling up after losses is the fastest way to empty a bankroll. Accept losses as part of the game.
  2. Staking too high on accumulators — treating an accumulator as a "sure thing" and staking your monthly bankroll on it.
  3. Mixing sports — spreading your analysis too thin across 5+ sports reduces quality. Focus on 1–2 sports where you have genuine knowledge.
  4. No stop-loss rule — professional bettors set a stop-loss: if the bankroll drops 30%, stop and reassess. Don't keep betting through a losing run.
