Breaking Sports News — Betting Market Signals

When major sports stories break, betting markets adjust within minutes. The fastest bettors can find genuine value in the brief window between a news event and a market adjustment.

How to Act on Sports News

  1. Verify before betting — a single unverified tweet has moved betting markets. Wait for a second source (official team statement, credible journalist confirmation) before placing
  2. Check if the market has already moved — compare current odds to pre-news prices. If prices haven't moved, you may have genuine value; if they've already shifted significantly, you're likely too late
  3. In-play vs pre-match — some news is better used in-play (where you can watch the impact unfold) rather than pre-match
