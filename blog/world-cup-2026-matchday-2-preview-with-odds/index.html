However, Cape Verde’s spirited performance in the second half suggests they could trouble Spain if the Europeans underestimate them.

FAQ

How do I bet on World Cup 2026 matches?

Choose a licensed African bookmaker like Bet9ja, SportyBet, Betway, or 1xBet. Navigate to the World Cup 2026 section, select your match, pick a market (e.g., match winner, over/under goals), and confirm your stake.

What do the odds numbers actually mean?

Decimal odds (common in Africa) show potential returns. For example, odds of 2.50 mean a ₦100 stake returns ₦250 (including your original stake) if the bet wins.

Can I bet on a draw at the World Cup?

Yes. The 3-way match winner market always includes the draw as a separate outcome.

What is live betting during a World Cup match?

Live betting allows you to place wagers after kickoff, with odds updating in real time based on match events.
