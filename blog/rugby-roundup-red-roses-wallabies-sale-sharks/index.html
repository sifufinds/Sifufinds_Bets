Dan Robson’s Return to Gloucester

The Guardian also brings us the story of Dan Robson, who’s returned to Gloucester after four years in France. Robson, a scrum-half, believes his time abroad has refreshed his outlook and that Gloucester can make a mark this season. This kind of player movement and renewed focus often influences team performance markets, so keep an eye on Gloucester’s odds as the season progresses.

Bristol Bears Secure Gabriel Oghre

In other Premiership news, Talking Rugby Union reports that Gabriel Oghre has signed a new deal with Bristol Bears. Oghre’s commitment to the club is a positive sign for Bristol’s long-term prospects. Bettors should watch how this stability affects their odds in both domestic and European competitions.

Georgie Perris-Redding’s Journey to the US Eagles

This is a player to watch, especially in women’s rugby markets, where the US Eagles are looking to make their mark.
