Licensing and Regulation

South Africa’s gambling market is overseen by provincial Gambling Boards – the WCGRB, NGB and ECGBB. These bodies enforce strict licensing conditions, ensuring that operators provide a safe environment for bettors. A licensed bookmaker must display its licence number and abide by the regulatory framework, including responsible gambling measures and consumer protection policies. When you see a WCGRB or NGB licence on a bookmaker’s site, you can be confident that they operate under local law.
