Licensing and regulation in Tanzania

All bookmakers listed except WinWinBet operate under a licence issued by the Gaming Board of Tanzania. The Board ensures that operators adhere to strict anti‑money‑laundering standards, protect player funds and provide transparent dispute‑resolution channels. A GBT‑licensed site must display its licence number, offer a clear privacy policy and allow players to set deposit limits. When a bookmaker is not GBT‑licensed, Tanzanian punters lose the guarantee of local consumer protection, and any dispute may need to be resolved under foreign jurisdiction.
