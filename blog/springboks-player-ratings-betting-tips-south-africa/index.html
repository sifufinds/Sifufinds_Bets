Licensing and Regulation in South Africa

South African gambling is overseen by a trio of authorities: the Western Cape Gambling Regulation Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). Each regulator issues licences that guarantee the operator adheres to strict responsible‑gaming, anti‑money‑laundering and consumer‑protection standards.

Hollywoodbets and BetXchange operate under WCGRB licences, meaning they are vetted by the provincial board that monitors all land‑based and online betting activities in the Western Cape. Supabets holds an NGB licence, giving it national reach and compliance with the National Gambling Act. When you see a bookmaker stating “WCGRB Licensed” or “NGB Licensed”, you can be confident that the site is legally permitted to accept South African players and that your funds are held in segregated accounts.

These licences also enforce the 18+ age requirement, mandatory FICA verification and clear dispute‑resolution channels. In case of a disagreement, you can approach the regulator directly, which adds an extra layer of security for your Springboks wagers.
