Licensing, regulation and why it matters for Tanzanian bettors

The Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT) is the statutory body responsible for overseeing all betting activities within the country. Every bookmaker operating in Tanzania must obtain a GBT licence, which obliges them to adhere to strict standards concerning player protection, anti‑money‑laundering (AML) procedures, and fair gaming. The bookmakers featured in this guide – 1xBet Tanzania, 22Bet Tanzania, Betika Tanzania, SportPesa Tanzania, FairPari Tanzania and Betway Tanzania – are all GBT Licensed, meaning they have passed rigorous checks and are monitored regularly.

Why should you care? A licensed operator is required to keep player funds in a separate, protected account, ensuring that your deposits cannot be used for anything other than betting. They must also provide transparent terms and conditions, including clear statements of wagering requirements, odds calculation methods and dispute resolution processes. In the event of a disagreement, the GBT offers a complaints handling mechanism that can intervene on your behalf.

Moreover, the regulator enforces responsible gambling obligations. Licensed operators must display tools for self‑exclusion, deposit limits and reality checks. By choosing a GBT‑licensed bookmaker you benefit from a legal safety net that unlicensed sites simply cannot provide.
