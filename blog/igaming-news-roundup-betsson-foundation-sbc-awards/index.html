iGaming News Roundup: Betsson Foundation, SBC Awards, and More

Eish, the iGaming world is buzzing with fresh developments, and African bettors need to stay in the loop! From philanthropic initiatives to regulatory changes and industry awards, here’s a breakdown of the latest news that could impact your betting experience. Let’s dive in!

Betsson Foundation: A New Era of Corporate Responsibility

As reported by SBC News just 3.3 hours ago, the Betsson Group has launched the Betsson Foundation, a dedicated platform for scaling its social impact activities. This move highlights the growing trend of iGaming companies stepping up their corporate responsibility game.

For African bettors, this is a positive sign. Companies like Betsson, which operate across platforms similar to Bet9ja and Sportybet, are increasingly focusing on sustainability and community welfare. While the foundation’s specific initiatives are yet to be announced, it’s the kind of development that could enhance trust in the industry.

Czech Government Blocks Kalshi: What It Means for African Bettors

In another SBC News update (4.1 hours ago), the Czech government has ordered internet service providers (ISPs) to block Kalshi, giving them less than two weeks to comply. This regulatory move underscores the global challenges faced by iGaming operators.

While this specific action doesn’t directly affect African markets, it’s a reminder of how quickly regulatory landscapes can shift. African bettors should always compare platforms like 1xBet and Betway to ensure they’re using licensed and secure services.

Genting Casinos Closes Coventry Venue: A Sign of the Times?

Genting Casinos, as per SBC News (8.7 hours ago), has announced the closure of its Coventry land-based venue. The company cited operational challenges as the primary reason.

This closure reflects the broader shift from physical casinos to online platforms, a trend that’s also evident in Africa. With mobile betting booming across Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa, platforms like Hollywoodbets and Betika are becoming the go-to choices for punters.

SBC Summit Lisbon: Locked in Until 2030

In a major announcement (10.1 hours ago), SBC has signed a long-term agreement with Turismo de Portugal, the Municipality of Lisbon, and Lisboa FCE to keep the SBC Summit in Lisbon until 2030.

This commitment ensures that Lisbon remains a hub for iGaming innovation, which could indirectly benefit African operators and bettors. Keep an eye on developments from this summit, as they often set the tone for global iGaming trends.

SBC Awards 2026: Celebrating Industry Excellence

The SBC Awards 2026 winners were announced 11.1 hours ago, with Kaizen Gaming, Pragmatic Play, Sportradar, and BetConstruct AI each bagging two awards. These companies are known for their cutting-edge solutions, which could soon make their way to African markets.

For bettors using platforms like Melbet and BetKing, this is a reminder to look for operators that partner with award-winning providers for a top-notch betting experience.

Reflex Gaming’s Fat Cats DoubleMax: A New Slot to Watch

Vegas Slots Online (11.2 hours ago) reports that Reflex Gaming is set to launch Fat Cats DoubleMax in October 2026, expanding its DoubleMax mechanic family.

While slots aren’t the primary focus for sports bettors, this launch highlights the innovation in iGaming. African punters can expect similar advancements in sports betting interfaces and features soon.

Finland’s Regulated Market: Lessons for Africa

iGaming Business (12.2 hours ago) explores the challenges of Finland’s regulated online market, where some operators start with millions of customers while others begin from scratch.

This imbalance is a cautionary tale for African regulators. As markets like Nigeria’s NPFL and South Africa’s PSL attract more betting activity, ensuring a level playing field will be crucial for sustainable growth.

G2E 2026: Wayne Gretzky Takes the Stage

The 2026 G2E Review (22.3 hours ago) featured hockey legend Wayne Gretzky and top industry CEOs at the world’s largest gambling conference. Gretzky’s presence underscores the intersection of sports and iGaming, a trend that’s gaining traction in Africa too.

With events like the CAF Champions League and AFCON drawing massive betting interest, African platforms are likely to invest more in sports-centric innovations.

Prediction Markets: The Next Big Thing?

On Day 3 of the SBC Summit (1.1 days ago), prediction markets were hailed as an “extraordinary opportunity in innovation,” though doubts remain about their implementation.

If these markets take off, African bettors could see new ways to wager on everything from election outcomes to weather events. Keep an eye on platforms like Bet9ja and Sportybet for potential updates.

Hacksaw Gaming’s Buried By Night: Horror Meets iGaming

Vegas Slots Online (1.2 days ago) highlights Hacksaw Gaming’s latest release, Buried By Night, a horror-themed slot using the DuelRows mechanic.

While slots aren’t the main focus for sports bettors, this launch showcases the creativity in iGaming. African platforms might soon incorporate similar themes into their sports betting interfaces.
