The 7 African Stars to Watch at World Cup 2026

According to BBC Sport Africa, these players are set to light up the tournament:

  1. Victor Osimhen (Nigeria) – The Napoli striker is one of the most lethal finishers in Europe, with his pace and power making him a nightmare for defenders.
  2. Sadio Mané (Senegal) – A proven performer on the big stage, Mané will be Senegal’s talisman as they aim to replicate their 2022 success.
  3. Achraf Hakimi (Morocco) – The PSG full-back’s attacking prowess and pinpoint crosses make him a key figure for Morocco.
  4. Thomas Partey (Ghana) – Arsenal’s midfield enforcer will be crucial in Ghana’s quest for World Cup glory.
  5. Mohamed Salah (Egypt) – Despite recent struggles, Salah remains Egypt’s most dangerous player and a constant threat.
  6. Naby Keita (Guinea) – The Liverpool midfielder’s creativity and vision could unlock defenses in the group stage.
  7. Kalidou Koulibaly (Senegal) – One of the world’s best center-backs, Koulibaly will anchor Senegal’s defense.
