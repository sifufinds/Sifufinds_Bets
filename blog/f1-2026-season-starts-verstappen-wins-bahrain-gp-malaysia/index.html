F1 2026 Season Kicks Off with Chaos in Malaysia: Verstappen Wins Rain-Hit Bahrain GP

Eish, the 2026 Formula 1 season has started with a bang—or should we say, a splash? The Bahrain Grand Prix, held at the Sepang International Circuit in Malaysia, delivered drama, chaos, and a thrilling race that left fans and bettors alike on the edge of their seats. Max Verstappen emerged victorious, but it wasn’t without its twists and turns. Let’s break down the key moments and what they mean for your F1 betting strategy.

Verstappen Conquers the Rain-Delayed Bahrain GP

As reported by Malay Mail, Max Verstappen claimed the first win of the 2026 F1 season in a rain-hit Bahrain Grand Prix. The Red Bull driver navigated the treacherous conditions at Sepang with precision, proving once again why he’s the reigning champion. Despite a delayed start due to heavy rain, Verstappen held off the competition to secure the top spot on the podium.

Hamilton Surges Back After Disastrous Tyre Choice

Lewis Hamilton, now racing for Ferrari, had a rollercoaster race. According to Sky Sports, Hamilton revealed a debate over tyre choice at the start of the race, which ultimately led to a disastrous beginning. However, the seven-time world champion showed his class by surging back through the field to secure a podium finish, thanks to George Russell’s late heartbreak.

Hamilton’s resilience is a reminder of his ability to bounce back, even in challenging conditions. This performance could influence his odds for future races and the championship. Check out markets across Bet9ja and Sportybet to see how his price has moved.

George Russell’s Heartbreak and Mercedes’ Reliability Woes

Mercedes’ George Russell looked set for a podium finish until a late issue forced him to retire, handing third place to teammate Hamilton. Sky Sports reports that Mercedes boss Toto Wolff is “massively annoyed” by the unreliability, with Russell expressing that he’s “beyond disappointment.”

This is a significant concern for Mercedes, who were expected to challenge for the title this season. Reliability issues can drastically impact a team’s championship chances, and bettors should keep a close eye on how this develops. FIA Explains ‘Unprecedented’ Glitch Causing Start Chaos

The race got off to a chaotic start due to an “unprecedented” software glitch, as explained by the FIA. Sky Sports reports that the issue affected multiple cars during the formation laps, with Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton likening the experience to a “Lego race,” as per BBC F1.

While the glitch didn’t directly impact the race result, it’s a reminder of the unpredictability of F1. Such incidents can influence betting markets, particularly in-play options. Keep an eye on live betting opportunities with Hollywoodbets and Betika during future races.

Liam Lawson Returns to the Points

Liam Lawson had a strong race, finishing seventh in the chaotic conditions. NZ Herald and VelocityNews highlight his impressive performance, which marks his return to the points after a challenging 2025 season.

Lawson’s form could make him a dark horse for future races, especially in unpredictable conditions. This is the kind of squad news that typically moves driver odds—compare the current live odds on SifuFinds before betting.

Antonelli Admits P2 Was ‘The Maximum’

Ferrari’s Antonelli secured second place but admitted that P2 was “the maximum” he could achieve, as Verstappen was simply too quick. Malay Mail reports that Antonelli acknowledged the gap between Red Bull and the rest of the field.

This admission could impact Ferrari’s constructor championship odds, as it suggests they may struggle to challenge Red Bull consistently. Compare the odds on Melbet and BetKing to see how the market reacts.
