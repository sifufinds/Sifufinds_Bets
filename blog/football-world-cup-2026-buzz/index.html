Real Madrid has made headlines with President Florentino Perez’s promise to make a club-record offer for an unnamed "great player" if re-elected. Foreign Investment in English Football

The English football regulator faces a "defining test" with Saudi Arabian official Turki Al-Sheikh’s potential investment in Derby County. This development has sparked debates about foreign ownership in English football, a topic that resonates with African fans familiar with similar investments in their local leagues.

The Professional Footballers’ Association (PFA) has announced its shortlists for the Men’s and Women’s Player of the Year awards. Manchester City’s Khadija ‘Bunny’ Shaw and Yui Hasegawa are among the contenders, showcasing the growing influence of African and global talent in the sport.

Betting Strategies for African Bettors

To maximize your betting success during the 2026 World Cup, consider these strategies:

#### 1. Leverage Squad News

Squad announcements are critical for betting markets. Follow local journalists and official team channels for early updates on injuries, suspensions, and key player inclusions. This information can give you an edge before bookmakers adjust their odds.

#### 2. Monitor Form and Tactics

Team form and tactical changes can significantly impact match outcomes. Keep an eye on pre-tournament friendlies and player performances in their respective leagues. For instance, a team with a strong defensive record might be a good bet for under 2.5 goals markets.

Player-specific markets, such as top goalscorer, assists, and player of the tournament, offer excellent value. African stars like Sadio Mané (Senegal) and Victor Osimhen (Nigeria) could be strong contenders for these awards.

African Football Betting: Value Strategies

African football bettors can gain an edge by focusing on local insights and trends:

#### 1. Home Advantage in African Leagues

Home teams in African leagues like the NPFL, KPL, and GPL have a significantly higher win rate compared to European leagues. This trend often extends to international fixtures, making home teams a valuable bet.

#### 2. Post-International Window Dip

Teams often experience a dip in performance after international breaks due to player fatigue. Monitor this pattern and consider betting against teams with key players returning from international duty.

#### 3. Weather and Pitch Conditions

African pitches vary widely in quality, and away teams from top clubs often struggle on poor surfaces. Factor in weather conditions and pitch quality when placing bets.
