Licensing: Why "Curaçao" Is Not the Whole Answer

An offshore Curaçao licence is common among international bookmakers. It is not the same as permission to operate in your country.

Local rules are strict in several of these markets. In Ghana, the Gaming Commission reportedly expects licence applicants to be at least partly Ghanaian-owned with substantial minimum capital. In Tanzania, the Gaming Board expects significant local investment from foreign-owned operators. Kenya's regulator changed in 2026, with one report saying the Gambling Regulatory Authority took over from the BCLB on 28 February 2026, so check the current register at gra.go.ke rather than trusting an old BCLB reference.

Egypt needs particular care. The sources we found disagree on whether online sports betting is lawful there, and we could not settle it. If you live in Egypt, check your own legal position before registering.

None of this means Mostbet is unsafe. It means the burden is on you to confirm that it may legally accept players where you live.

Practical advice that applies to any new bookmaker:

  • Verify your ID before you need to withdraw, not after you win.
