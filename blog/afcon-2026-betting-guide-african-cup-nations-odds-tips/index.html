Analysis: The AFCON 2026 Contenders

Morocco — Favourites and With Good Reason

Morocco's World Cup 2022 semi-final run and consistent FIFA ranking improvement since 2020 makes them the clear outright favourite. Coach Walid Regragui has built a tactically disciplined side with world-class individual quality. Home continent advantage — though they play in East Africa, not North — won't be a factor. Their quality will be.

Senegal — The Team to Beat

AFCON 2023 champions Senegal bring the best squad in their history. Sadio Mané, Idrissa Gueye, Ismaila Sarr, and Pape Matar Sarr form one of Africa's most complete midfield-attack combinations. Senegal have the quality to win back-to-back AFCON titles.

Nigeria — Inconsistent But Dangerous

Nigeria should always be considered in AFCON betting given their talent pool. The Super Eagles have underperformed their potential at recent tournaments but have the attacking quality (Osimhen, Lookman, Chukwueze) to beat anyone on their day.

Ivory Coast — Defending Champions

Les Éléphants won AFCON 2023 on home soil in dramatic fashion. As defending champions on African soil, they carry extra motivation. Sébastien Haller, Franck Kessié, and Nicolas Pépé provide the quality. Can they retain?

AFCON 2026 Group Stage Betting Guide

Group Stage Format

AFCON 2026 features 24 teams in 6 groups of 4. The top 2 from each group (12 teams) plus the 4 best third-placed teams (16 total) advance to the Round of 16.

Group Stage Betting Strategy

  1. Back group winners early — odds are best before group draws are announced
  2. First match importance — teams that win their opening game advance from the group 78% of the time historically at AFCON
  3. Penalty shootout insurance — AFCON knockout games go to penalties more frequently than European tournaments. Hedging with draw + penalties markets has value

Key Group Stage Picks

  • Morocco to win their group @2.20 — The Atlas Lions dominate their group in most draw scenarios
  • Senegal to advance from group @1.15 — Near certainty given squad quality

AFCON 2026 — Betting Patterns From History

Host nation advantage: Co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania — East African teams (if qualified) may benefit from crowd support. Kenya have qualified and home advantage is a genuine factor.

Top scorer betting: The AFCON top scorer is rarely from a top-4 nation. Value often lies in strikers from smaller nations who face weaker opponents in the group stage. Watch for value at @15.00+ on players from Ivory Coast, Mali, or Cameroon.

Tournament structure: The expanded 24-team format since 2019 means more weaker nations qualify. Upsets in the group stage are more common — back draws between ranked nations against underdog sides for value.
