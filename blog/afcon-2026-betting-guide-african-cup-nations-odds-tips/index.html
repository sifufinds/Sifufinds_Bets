AFCON 2026 — Full Tournament Guide

Tournament Format and Dates

AFCON 2026 features 24 teams divided into 6 groups of 4. The top 2 from each group plus the 4 best third-placed teams advance to the Round of 16. The tournament concludes with a final in the host country.

Historical Performance Analysis — Picking Winners

In the last 10 AFCON tournaments, the winner has come from just 4 nations: Egypt (4 titles), Senegal, Ivory Coast, and Cameroon. Morocco have never won AFCON despite consistently fielding competitive squads — their best finish was runners-up. This history gives context to their WC2026 favourite odds.
