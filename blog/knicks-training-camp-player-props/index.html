3. Player Prop Betting – The Basics

A player prop bet is a wager on a specific statistical category for a single player in a single game. Common categories include:

  • Points – e.g., “Shamet > 10.5 points.”
  • Rebounds – e.g., “Shamet < 4.5 rebounds.”
  • Assists – e.g., “Shamet > 2.5 assists.”
  • Combined stats – e.g., “Points + Rebounds + Assists > 15.5.”

Unlike standard moneyline or point‑spread bets, player props are purely statistical. They are highly sensitive to minute allocation and game context, making them a favorite for bettors who enjoy data‑driven play.
