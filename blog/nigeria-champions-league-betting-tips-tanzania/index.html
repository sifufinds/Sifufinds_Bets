Licensing, regulation and safety in Tanzania

All the bookmakers listed operate under a licence issued by the Gaming Board of Tanzania. By choosing a GBT‑licensed site you benefit from a legal framework that allows you to lodge complaints, request audits of odds and receive guaranteed payouts. The board also mandates responsible gambling tools, such as deposit limits and self‑exclusion, which are built into each platform’s dashboard.
