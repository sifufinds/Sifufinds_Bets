The Wagering Rules That Decide Whether It Is Worth It

A big percentage means little without the rollover. The third-party review reports these conditions:

  • Wager the bonus amount 12 times.
  • Bets must be accumulators with at least three selections.
  • Each selection needs odds of 2.10 or higher.
  • Use the bonus within 30 days or lose it, along with winnings from it.

Here is what that means in practice, using Kenya as an example. Deposit KSh 1,000 and a 200% match adds KSh 2,000 of bonus. Twelve times the bonus is KSh 24,000 in qualifying accumulator stakes. Over 30 days that is roughly KSh 800 a day, on bets that need three legs each at 2.10 or better. Accumulators with those odds lose far more often than they win, which is why most players never clear a bonus this size.

The honest takeaway is to size your deposit for the bets you were going to place anyway. If you are not comfortable staking that volume, a smaller deposit with the same percentage is the better choice.
