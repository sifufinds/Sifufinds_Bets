Betting Markets Beyond the Match Winner

Following this story, there are several tennis‑specific markets worth knowing beyond simply picking the winner. The Total Games market sets a line for the combined number of games played across the whole match, letting you bet over or under regardless of who wins — useful in matches expected to go the distance or be tightly contested on serve.

Live, point‑by‑point betting is widely supported by African bookmakers during Grand Slam fortnight, letting you react to a player losing the opening set, saving break points, or building momentum on serve.
