Bettors can therefore weigh a lower‑than‑average over/under goal line against United’s next opponent, especially if Carrick opts for a back‑to‑basics 4‑2‑3‑1. Conversely, a manager under fire may gamble with an aggressive forward line to restore confidence, which would favour markets like Manchester United to win to nil or Anytime Goal for their attacking stars.
