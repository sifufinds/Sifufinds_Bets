FAQ

Is online betting legal in South Africa?

Yes, but only with a bookmaker licensed by a South African provincial gambling board. The National Gambling Act 7 of 2004 makes it unlawful to gamble with an unlicensed operator, including offshore international sites that aren't registered with a South African regulator.

Which regulator licenses the most South African bookmakers?

The Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB) issues the largest number of online bookmaker licences and permits the broadest range of betting products, though other provincial boards such as the NGB and Gauteng Gambling Board also license operators.

How can I tell if a betting site is illegal in South Africa?

Licensed operators must publish their licence number, trading entity name, and licensing provincial authority — usually in the site footer, About Us page, or terms and conditions. If a site shows no South African licensing information, treat it as unlicensed and avoid depositing.

What are the penalties for using illegal gambling sites in South Africa?

Under the National Gambling Act, operators — and even payment processors and ISPs who facilitate access to unlicensed gambling sites — can face fines of up to R10 million or up to 10 years imprisonment, a strong incentive for South African bettors to stick to WCGRB/NGB-licensed platforms.
