Match background and recent form

Zimbabwe entered their last three internationals with a record of one win, one draw and one loss. Their solitary victory came against a lower‑ranked side, but they managed to keep a clean sheet in that game, highlighting a disciplined defensive structure under coach Zdravko Logar. The team’s leading scorer, Knowledge Musona, has only found the net once in the past six matches, suggesting a potential dip in attacking potency.

RDC, on the other hand, have been on a modest upward trajectory. In their previous four games they have secured two wins, one draw and one loss. Their midfield engine, Chancel Mbemba, has contributed three assists and two goals, indicating a player capable of influencing the final third. Moreover, the Congolese defence has conceded just one goal in the last five fixtures, a statistic that cannot be ignored when assessing over/under markets.

Both sides will likely line up in a 4‑3‑3 formation, but Zimbabwe may opt for a more compact 4‑5‑1 to deny space in midfield. The tactical battle will therefore hinge on whether Zimbabwe can disrupt the fluid passing of RDC or whether the Congolese side can exploit the wings with pacey forwards such as Cédric Bakambu.

Licensing and regulation in Cameroon

All reputable operators serving Cameroonian bettors must hold a licence from the Autorité des Marchés des Jeux (AMJ). The AMJ monitors compliance with responsible gambling standards, ensures that operators maintain sufficient financial reserves, and enforces anti‑money‑laundering measures. When you sign up with a bookmaker, look for the AMJ licence badge on the website footer. This badge guarantees that the operator is subject to regular audits and that your deposits are protected under Cameroonian law.

Linebet operates under a Curaçao licence, which is not issued by the AMJ. While Curaçao‑licensed sites are legal in many jurisdictions, they are not overseen by the Cameroonian regulator. If you value local consumer protection, it is advisable to prioritise AMJ‑licensed bookmakers such as 1xBet, PMUC, Melbet, FairPari and BetPawa.
