Scotland’s 28‑Year Return: A Tale of Hope and History

Scotland’s re‑entry into the World Cup after a 28‑year absence has ignited excitement across the UK and beyond. Their squad, led by head coach Derek McInnes, is a blend of seasoned internationals like John McGinn and emerging talents such as Duncan Ferguson. Historically, Scotland has a respectable World Cup record: they reached the quarter‑finals in 1998 and the round of 16 in 2002. However, their recent campaigns have been less successful, making this tournament a crucial opportunity to rebuild their legacy.
