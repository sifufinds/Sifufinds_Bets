Choose a licensed African bookmaker such as Bet9ja, SportyBet, Betway, or 1xBet, navigate to the World Cup 2026 section, select your match, pick a market like match winner, over/under goals, or both teams to score, then confirm your stake. Odds on African platforms are almost always shown in decimal format and update in real time as team news and lineups are confirmed.

What do the odds numbers actually mean?

Most African bookmakers use decimal odds — a price of 1.80 means a ₦100 or KSh100 stake returns ₦180/KSh180 total (including your original stake) if the bet wins. The lower the decimal number, the shorter the odds and the stronger the favourite is considered by the bookmaker.

Can I bet on a draw at the World Cup?

Yes. Unlike most other sports, football always includes the draw as a separate outcome on the standard 3-way match winner market. If you back a team to win outright and the match finishes level, that specific bet loses unless you backed the draw itself or used a double-chance market covering it.

What is live betting during a World Cup match?

Live (in-play) betting lets you place wagers after kickoff, with odds updating continuously as goals, cards, and momentum shift. It's popular for reacting to a team going behind or a red card changing the shape of the game, and most major African bookmakers support live streaming alongside in-play markets for World Cup 2026 fixtures.
