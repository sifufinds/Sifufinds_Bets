FAQ

What are the best betting markets for the 2026 World Cup group stages?

The draw and over/under markets often provide good value, especially in three‑team groups where a single result can change the standings.

How does a new coach affect a team’s odds?

A new coach can introduce tactical uncertainty, which may widen the odds for the favorite and create opportunities for upset or draw bets.

Should I bet on VAR‑related incidents?

VAR controversies can shift odds slightly, but the impact is usually marginal. Look for small deviations and consider them in your overall strategy.

Is it safe to bet on underdog teams in major tournaments?

Underdogs can win, especially early in a tournament. However, they often carry higher risk, so balance your portfolio with safer bets.
