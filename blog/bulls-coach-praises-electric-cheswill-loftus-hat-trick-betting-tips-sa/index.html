Licensing and regulation in South Africa

South African online betting is overseen by several provincial bodies – the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). These regulators enforce strict standards on player protection, fair play and responsible gambling. Operators that display a WCGRB or NGB licence have undergone rigorous checks, meaning your funds are held in segregated accounts and disputes are handled by an independent authority.
