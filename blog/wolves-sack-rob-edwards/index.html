Wolves have sacked head coach Rob Edwards after just seven months in charge following their relegation from the Premier League. This news has sent shockwaves through the football community, and fans are eagerly waiting to see who will take over as the new manager.

According to reports, Wolves are close to appointing Portuguese manager César Peixoto, who led Gil Vicente to sixth place in the Segunda Liga. This move is expected to bring some much‑needed stability to the team, and fans are hoping that Peixoto will be able to lead the team back to the Premier League.

Edwards’ tenure began on 8 January 2025, a day after Wolves were relegated. In his 28 competitive games, he managed only one win and suffered 5 draws, a record that left the club’s supporters and boardroom uneasy. In contrast, the team’s previous two seasons under Nuno Gomes saw a steady climb from 14th to 10th, with a win rate of 44 % and 63 points. The stark difference underscores why the club felt an urgent change was necessary.

César Peixoto’s résumé at Gil Vicente is equally compelling. In the 2024‑25 Segunda Liga, he guided the club to a sixth‑place finish, achieving 11 wins from 18 matches in the first half of the season. His tactical approach focuses on high‑pressing possession football, a system that could blend well with the young, energetic Wolves squad that already boasts talents such as Pedro Sanchez and Callum Ferguson. If Peixoto is confirmed, the club will be the first Premier League side to hire a coach whose primary experience is in the second tier of Portuguese football—a bold but potentially rewarding gamble.
