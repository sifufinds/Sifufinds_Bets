Licensing and Regulation in South Africa

South African betting is overseen by a trio of provincial bodies: the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling and Racing Board (ECGBB). Each bookmaker listed above holds a licence from at least one of these regulators. Hollywoodbets and BetXchange operate under WCGRB licences, while Supabets is NGB licensed. These licences guarantee that the operators comply with strict consumer protection standards, fair‑play auditing and anti‑money‑laundering measures.
