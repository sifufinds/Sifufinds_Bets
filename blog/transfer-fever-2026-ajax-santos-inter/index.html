Responsible Gambling

Football transfers can be thrilling, but they’re also volatile. Here are a few reminders:

  1. Set a Budget – Decide how much you’re willing to risk before you place a bet.
  2. Stay Informed – Use reliable sources (e.g., Transfermarkt, official club statements) to avoid chasing rumours.
  3. Know the Market – Transfer bets have higher variance than match outcome bets.
  4. Take Breaks – Don’t chase losses; step away if you’re feeling pressured.

If you or someone you know is struggling with gambling, seek help from local support organisations or international hotlines.
