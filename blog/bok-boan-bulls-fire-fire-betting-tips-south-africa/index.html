Licensing and regulation in South Africa

South Africa’s gambling landscape is overseen by three bodies: the Western Cape Gambling Regulation Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). Operators that carry a WCGRB or NGB licence are required to adhere to strict consumer‑protection standards, including transparent odds calculation, responsible‑gaming tools and prompt dispute resolution. Internationally licensed sites such as BetWinner, Melbet and Paripesa are permitted to operate under the country’s liberalised online gambling framework, but they must still respect the local age limit of 18 and the anti‑money‑laundering rules enforced by the Financial Intelligence Centre. When you pick a bookmaker, verify that the licence badge is displayed on the homepage and that the regulator’s name is mentioned in the footer.
