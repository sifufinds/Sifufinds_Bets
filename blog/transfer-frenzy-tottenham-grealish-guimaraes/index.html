7. FAQ – Quick Answers

Q: How long does a transfer negotiation usually take?

It can vary from a few days to several weeks, depending on medicals, release clauses, and both clubs’ urgency.

Q: Can I bet on a transfer that hasn’t happened yet?

Yes – many bookmakers offer “transfer market” markets, betting on whether a player will sign or not, or on the final fee bracket.
