The cancellation was prompted by Spanish authorities, who raised alarms about the potential health risks associated with hosting the Congolese team amid the ongoing Ebola situation.

According to reports from BBC Sport and Guardian Football, the Spanish city set to host the match decided to call off the fixture as a precautionary measure. This decision is a significant setback for DR Congo, who were counting on this friendly to fine-tune their squad and tactics ahead of the World Cup.
