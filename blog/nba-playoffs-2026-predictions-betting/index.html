Analysis: Boston Celtics — Experience and Depth

The Celtics bring playoff-hardened veterans, a deep bench, and one of the best defensive systems in the NBA. Their ability to switch on defence and create three-point looks at the other end makes them structurally sound against any opponent.

Key players: Jayson Tatum (averaging 29 points in these playoffs), Jaylen Brown, and Kristaps Porzingis when healthy. Boston's depth off the bench has been exceptional throughout the playoff run.

Weakness: Three-point variance. When the shots aren't falling, Boston can go cold for extended stretches.

---

Analysis: Oklahoma City Thunder — Youth and Athleticism

OKC are the most athletic team in these playoffs. Their transition game is devastating, and their defensive pressure forces turnovers at a higher rate than any other team. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has been the most complete player in the playoffs — 32 points, 6 assists per game.

Key players: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Chet Holmgren, and Josh Giddey creating mismatches across the court.

Weakness: Playoff experience. This is a young team in the Finals for the first time.
