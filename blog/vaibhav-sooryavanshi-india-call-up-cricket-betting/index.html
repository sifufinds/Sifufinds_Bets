IPL vs International Cricket: Betting Strategies

The IPL and international cricket require different betting approaches due to their distinct formats:

  • IPL (T20): Fast-paced with high-scoring matches. Popular markets include sixes, team totals, and powerplay runs. High variance makes it ideal for risk-takers.
  • International Cricket (Tests/ODIs): More strategic and analytical. Session betting and wicket markets offer value for informed bettors. The draw is a genuine outcome in Tests.
  • T20 Internationals: A balance between T20 and ODI formats. Match winner and team totals are the most popular markets.
