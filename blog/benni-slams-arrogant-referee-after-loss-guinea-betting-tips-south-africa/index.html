Licensing and regulation in South Africa

South African betting is overseen by three main bodies: the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling and Racing Board (ECGBB). These regulators enforce strict licensing conditions, including mandatory responsible‑gaming measures, anti‑money‑laundering checks and dispute‑resolution channels. Bookmakers that display an NGB or WCGRB licence, such as FairPari and Betway, are required to keep player funds in separate trust accounts, providing an extra layer of security. Internationally licensed operators like Melbet and BetWinner are permitted to operate as long as they meet the South African technical and financial standards, but they are not directly supervised by the provincial boards. When choosing a platform, verify the licence badge on the homepage and read the regulator’s name in the footer.
