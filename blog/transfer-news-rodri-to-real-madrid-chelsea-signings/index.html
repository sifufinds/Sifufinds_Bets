Rodri's move to Real Madrid is gaining real pace, and he is not the only midfielder on the move this window. Chelsea have also been busy, agreeing a fee for a new signing and lining up a move for Manchester City's Pedro Neto.

Rodri to Real Madrid: where things stand

Rodri's switch to the Santiago Bernabeu looks close to being finalised. Manchester City's Ballon d'Or winner would slot straight into a Real Madrid midfield already chasing silverware on every front, and his positional discipline is exactly the profile Carlo Ancelotti's successors have wanted since Casemiro left for Manchester United. For African bettors, a deal of this size moves markets fast: Real Madrid's La Liga and Champions League prices typically shorten within days of a marquee signing being confirmed, so this is a window worth watching rather than one to react to after the fact.

Chelsea's business this window

Chelsea have not been standing still either. The Blues have agreed a £52m deal for a new signing, with a medical already booked, and they are also chasing Manchester City target Pedro Neto. Todd Boehly's recruitment strategy under the current regime has leaned on volume and youth, so a marquee arrival like this signals intent to compete on multiple fronts rather than simply rebuild for the future. Chelsea's Premier League and Champions League odds reflect a squad still finding its identity, but a strong finish to the transfer window could see those prices tighten.

Other deals worth watching

Crystal Palace have submitted a £21.3m bid for defender Chrislain Matsima, a move that would strengthen a backline already among the Premier League's most improved over the past two seasons. Celtic, meanwhile, have agreed a record fee for Kaoru Mitoma, a statement signing that underlines the scale of their ambitions domestically and in Europe. Neither deal moves outright title markets the way Rodri's or Chelsea's do, but both are worth tracking if you follow player-specific or top-four finish markets.
