Source: Sky Sports (6 minutes ago)

What this means for the odds

2️⃣ Danny Welbeck’s surprise Chelsea medical

Source: Sky Sports (9.6 hours ago)

Chelsea have granted veteran striker Danny Welbeck permission to undergo a medical, signalling a “surprise” move to Stamford Bridge. The 32‑year‑old, who left Brighton on a free, brings Premier League experience and a proven goal‑scoring record against top sides.

Odds impact

  • Chelsea: After a shaky start to the season, the Blues are now seen as a late‑season surge team. Welbeck adds depth to a forward line that has struggled with injuries. Chelsea’s odds to finish in the top‑four improve from 6.00 (Bet9ja) to 5.20 (Betway). Their relegation odds, already low, slip further from 70.00 to 80.00 – a sign of growing confidence.

African bettors who favour underdogs can look at Brighton’s rising odds as a potential value bet for a mid‑season “upset” market.
