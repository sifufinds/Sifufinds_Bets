Licensing and Regulation

All the bookmakers featured in this guide operate under a licence issued by the Betting Control and Licensing Board (BCLB). Responsible Gambling

Betting should be viewed as entertainment, not a way to solve financial problems. The BCLB requires every licensed operator to provide tools such as deposit limits, self‑exclusion and reality checks. If you feel you are chasing losses, take a break and use the self‑exclusion feature offered by Betika, SportPesa or 1xBet. Contact local support organisations such as the Kenya National Authority for the Prevention of Alcohol and Drug Abuse (KNAPDA) for counselling. Always gamble with money you can afford to lose and never chase a bonus to meet wagering requirements.
