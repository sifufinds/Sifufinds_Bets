Licensing, regulation and why it matters in Nigeria

The National Lottery Regulatory Commission (NLRC) is the body that oversees all gambling activities in Nigeria. Operators that hold an NLRC licence, such as 1xBet Nigeria, 22Bet Nigeria, Melbet Nigeria, Paripesa Nigeria and Sportybet Nigeria, must adhere to strict standards on player protection, data security and fair play. Choosing an NLRC‑licensed bookmaker gives you access to the regulator’s complaint handling service, ensuring that any issues with bonuses, withdrawals or account verification are dealt with promptly.
