Two very different transfers, at very different levels of the game, both completed within days of each other this summer.

Dortmund Land Genk's Wonderkid

Borussia Dortmund agreed a deal worth a reported €30m base fee, rising to €32m with add-ons, for Genk's 18-year-old Greek midfielder Konstantinos Karetsas. The agreement was reached on 27 July 2026 and the move completed on 3 August 2026, with Karetsas signing a five-year contract. It is the largest sale in Genk's history — the teenager played 92 matches for the Belgian club, scoring six goals and providing 23 assists, 14 of them last season alone.

Tom Ince Drops to League Two

At the other end of the market, winger Tom Ince joined League Two side Tranmere Rovers on a free transfer on 27 July 2026, signing a two-year deal after leaving Championship club Watford at the end of his contract. Ince, who has previously played for Liverpool, Crystal Palace, Hull City and Derby County among others, made 81 appearances for Watford across three seasons.
