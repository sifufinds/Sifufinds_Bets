The tournament format in a nutshell

UEFA keeps the competition structure familiar. Thirty‑six clubs are split into twelve groups of three. Each team plays four group games – two at home and two away – before the top two progress to the round of 16. From there the knockout rounds are two‑legged ties until the single‑match final in Istanbul. Knowing the format helps you spot value in early‑stage markets such as group winners, total goals and first‑goal scorer bets.

How the rankings translate into betting ideas

  1. Group winners – Back the top‑ranked side in each group for the “Group Winner” market. The odds are usually short but the probability is high, especially for City, Bayern, PSG and Real.
  2. Both teams to score (BTTS) – Teams with attacking depth (Manchester City, Liverpool, Dortmund) are prime for BTTS. Defensive stalwarts (Juventus, Atletico) are better for “No BTTS”.
  3. Over/Under goals – Expect over‑2.5 goals in matches involving Barcelona, Liverpool or Dortmund. Expect under‑2.5 in games featuring Juventus or Atletico.
  4. First goal scorer – Keep an eye on prolific forwards – Erling Haaland (Manchester City), Kylian Mbappé (Paris Saint‑Germain), Karim Benzema (Real Madrid). Their odds are often generous in the early rounds.
  5. Live betting – The Champions League often swings momentum after the 60th minute. Look for live “Next goal” or “Next corner” markets when a dominant side is ahead.
