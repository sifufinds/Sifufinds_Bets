For Ghanaian punters this result is more than a headline – it is a fresh betting narrative. The win signals a team that can start strong, keep clean sheets and find the net from wide areas. Why the Champion of Champions win matters for bettors

The Champion of Champions cup pits the league winners against the FA Cup holders, meaning the quality on display is close to that of a CAF competition. Medeama’s 2‑0 victory shows two things that matter to the betting market:

  1. Defensive solidity – keeping a clean sheet against Nations FC suggests the back line can handle pressure, a key factor when they face the physically imposing Mazembe side.
  2. Goal‑scoring from the flanks – Annor’s strike came from a wide‑area move, indicating that the wing‑back system will continue to produce chances.

Both points translate into specific markets: Both Teams to Score (BTTS) in the first leg, Under 2.5 Goals for a tight defensive display, and First Goal Scorer bets that favour Sulemana or Annor.

Early‑warning betting angles ahead of the Mazembe test

1. First‑leg result – Home win or draw

Medeama will host Mazembe for the first leg. Their recent form, combined with home advantage at Tarkwa, makes a home win or draw a sensible selection. The market usually offers decent odds because Mazembe’s reputation drives bookmakers to price the tie tighter.

2. Both Teams to Score – Yes

Mazembe possess a potent attack, while Medeama have shown they can breach any defence through the wings. Expect at least one goal from each side, especially if the game opens up after the first 20 minutes.

3. Over/Under 2.5 Goals – Under

Given Medeama’s clean‑sheet performance and the tactical caution that often accompanies a first‑leg African clash, many bettors prefer the under 2.5 goals line. A 0‑0 or 1‑0 result is a realistic scenario that also keeps the BTTS market alive.

4. First Goal Scorer – Sulemana or Annor

Both players are on the scoresheet already this season. Their positions allow them to arrive late in the box, making them attractive options for a first‑goal‑scorer market.

5. Anytime Goal Scorer – Annor

Annor’s pace and cutting‑in ability make him a constant threat. An anytime goal‑scorer bet on him often carries a lower price than the outright top‑scorer market but still offers decent returns.
