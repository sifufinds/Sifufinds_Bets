Check multiple reputable sources. If both Yahoo Soccer Transfers and a major outlet like Football365 report the same detail, the chance is higher. Beware of “fake‑news” feuds – they often cause odds to swing wildly.

Some African bookmakers (e.g., 1xBet) offer “over/under fee” markets. The current line for Souza’s fee is £20 m; Bet9ja’s over‑£20 m odds sit at 9/2.
