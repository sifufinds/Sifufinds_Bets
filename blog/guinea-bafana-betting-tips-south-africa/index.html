Licensing, regulation and player protection in South Africa

South African gambling is overseen by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). All three bookmakers listed hold a licence from either the WCGRB or NGB, meaning they must adhere to strict responsible‑gaming policies, anti‑money‑laundering checks and fair‑play standards. The provincial boards also enforce a cap on advertising to minors and require operators to display clear information about dispute resolution. When you bet with a licensed operator, you benefit from a legal framework that safeguards your deposits and winnings.
