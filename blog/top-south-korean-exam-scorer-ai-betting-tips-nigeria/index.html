Licensing, regulation and why it matters in Nigeria

The National Lottery Regulatory Commission (NLRC) is the body that licences and monitors gambling activities in Nigeria. An NLRC licence signals that the operator adheres to strict standards for player protection, data security and fair play. When you gamble with an NLRC‑licensed site such as 22Bet, 1xBet, Melbet, Paripesa or Linebet, you benefit from a local dispute‑resolution mechanism and the assurance that the operator has undergone background checks. Curaçao‑licensed bookmakers are permitted to operate in Nigeria, but they are regulated offshore and may not offer the same level of consumer recourse. Always check the licence information on the bookmaker’s footer before you sign up.
