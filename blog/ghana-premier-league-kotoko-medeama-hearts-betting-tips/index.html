Licensing and Regulation in Ghana

All the bookmakers listed operate under the authority of the Gaming Commission of Ghana (GCA). The GCA ensures that operators meet strict standards for player protection, fair play and financial transparency. A GCA licence means the bookmaker must keep player funds in separate accounts, conduct regular audits and provide a clear dispute‑resolution process. When you see “GCA Licensed” next to a bookmaker’s name, you can trust that the platform complies with Ghanaian law and is monitored by the regulator.
