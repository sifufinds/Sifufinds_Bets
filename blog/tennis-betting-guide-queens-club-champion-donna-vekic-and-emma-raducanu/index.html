FAQ

How do tennis betting odds work at Wimbledon and Queen's Club?

Tennis odds reflect a player's implied chance of winning the match — a shorter decimal price such as 1.50 marks the strong favourite, while a longer price like 4.50 marks the underdog. African bookmakers display these as decimal odds, so multiplying your stake by the decimal figure shows your total potential return including your stake.

What is a set betting market?

Set betting lets you predict the exact scoreline of a match in sets, such as 3-0, 3-1, or 3-2 for a best-of-five men's Grand Slam match at Wimbledon, or 2-0/2-1 for best-of-three women's matches and ATP 500 events like Queen's Club.

What does the Total Games over/under market mean?

This market sets a line for the combined number of games played across the whole match, and you bet on whether the actual total finishes over or under that number — useful for matches expected to go to tiebreaks or be tightly contested.

Can I bet on tennis matches while they're in progress?

Yes. Live tennis betting updates odds point-by-point and game-by-game, letting you react to a player losing the first set or picking up momentum on serve. Most licensed African bookmakers support live tennis markets throughout the grass-court season.
