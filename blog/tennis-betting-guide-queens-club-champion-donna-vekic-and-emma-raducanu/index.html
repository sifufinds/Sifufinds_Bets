FAQ

How do tennis betting odds work at Wimbledon and Queen's Club?

Tennis odds reflect a player’s implied chance of winning; a shorter decimal price like 1.50 marks the favourite, while a longer price such as 4.50 marks the underdog.

What is a set betting market?

Set betting lets you predict the exact set scoreline, such as 2‑0 or 2‑1 in women’s best‑of‑three matches.

What does the Total Games over/under market mean?

You bet on whether the combined number of games in a match will be over or under a set line, useful for matches expected to go to a tiebreak.

Can I bet on tennis matches while they're in progress?

Yes, live tennis betting updates odds point‑by‑point and game‑by‑game, allowing you to react to momentum shifts during the match.
