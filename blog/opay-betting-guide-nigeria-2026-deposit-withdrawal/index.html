Security & Compliance

OPay operates under the National Payments System (NPS) regulations and holds a Nigeria Bank of Deposit license. Key security features include:

  • Two‑factor authentication via OPay PIN and biometric data.
  • Real‑time fraud monitoring that flags irregular transfer patterns.
  • BVN (Bank Verification Number) verification for higher‑limit users, ensuring identity matching across all transactions.

Because OPay’s virtual accounts are tied to your phone number, the risk of mis‑directed funds is minimal. Betting operators can instantly verify your OPay transaction reference, reducing payout disputes.
