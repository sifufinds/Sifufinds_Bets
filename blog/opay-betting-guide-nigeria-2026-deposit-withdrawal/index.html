How to Deposit with OPay at a Nigerian Betting Site

Step-by-Step OPay Deposit

  1. Open the OPay app on your Android or iOS device
  2. Select "Transfer" from the home screen
  3. Select "To Business" or search for your bookmaker by name
  4. Enter your betting account number as the recipient account
  5. Enter the deposit amount (minimum ₦100 at most sites)
  6. Review the transaction details and confirm with your OPay PIN or fingerprint
  7. Your betting account is credited instantly

How to Deposit at a Nigerian Betting Site Using OPay

Method 1: OPay App → Bookmaker Wallet

  1. Open your bookmaker's app or website
  2. Go to Cashier → Deposit
  3. Select OPay or Fintech as payment method
  4. Enter the amount (min ₦100 at most sites)
  5. Enter your OPay phone number
  6. Approve the OPay payment request on your phone

Method 2: Bank Transfer to OPay Virtual Account

  1. Select Bank Transfer in the betting site cashier
  2. Choose OPay as the bank (account number is your OPay phone number)
  3. Transfer from any other bank app to your OPay virtual account
  4. Then initiate the deposit from OPay to the betting site

FAQs — OPay Betting Nigeria

Q: Why was my OPay withdrawal rejected by Bet9ja?

The most common reason is account name mismatch. Your Bet9ja account name must exactly match your OPay registered name. Middle names, initials, and spelling differences will cause rejection.

Q: What is the OPay daily withdrawal limit for betting?

OPay personal accounts have a ₦500,000 daily transfer limit. OPay Business accounts can transfer up to ₦3,000,000 daily. Most bookmakers also apply their own daily limits.

Q: Is OPay better than PalmPay for Nigerian betting withdrawals?

Both are fast. SportyBet processes both in under 5 minutes. OPay has slightly higher daily limits and more bookmaker integrations. PalmPay processes some specific bookmakers (like 22Bet) faster than OPay.
