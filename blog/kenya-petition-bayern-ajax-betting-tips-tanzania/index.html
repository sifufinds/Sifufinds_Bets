Licensing and Regulation in Tanzania

All the bookmakers listed above operate under the oversight of the Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT). The board ensures that operators adhere to strict standards for player protection, fair play and anti‑money‑laundering measures. A GBT licence means that the bookmaker must keep player funds in a segregated account, provide transparent terms and conditions, and submit regular audit reports. When you bet with a GBT‑licensed site, you have recourse to the regulator should a dispute arise.
