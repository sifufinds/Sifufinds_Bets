Licensing and Regulation

All bookmakers featured in this guide hold a BCLB licence, the official regulator for sports betting in Kenya. The BCLB enforces strict rules on fair play, data protection and responsible gambling. It also ensures that payouts are made promptly and that the odds are fair and transparent. If a bookmaker does not display its licence number, it is not authorised to operate in Kenya and should be avoided.

Payment Methods in Kenya

The most common payment channels for Kenyan bettors are:

  • M‑Pesa – Instant, widely accepted for deposits and withdrawals.
  • Airtel Money – Another instant option, especially popular among the youth.
  • Equitel – Less common but still available on a few platforms.
  • USSD *644# – Quick mobile top‑up, mainly used for low‑value transactions.
  • Bank Transfer – Used by some bookmakers for larger deposits or withdrawals.
  • Visa & Mastercard – Accepted by several bookmakers, offering an alternative to mobile money.

Choosing a bookmaker that supports multiple methods gives you flexibility and reduces the risk of delays.

Responsible Gambling

Betting should remain a form of entertainment, not a source of financial stress. The BCLB mandates that all bookmakers display responsible gambling resources, such as self‑exclusion tools and deposit limits. If you feel you are betting more than you can afford, use the “self‑exclusion” feature or seek help from local support groups. Remember, gambling can be addictive, and it is essential to gamble responsibly.
