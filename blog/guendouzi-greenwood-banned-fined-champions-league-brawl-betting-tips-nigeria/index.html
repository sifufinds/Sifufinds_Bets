Licensing and Regulation in Nigeria

Operating legally in Nigeria requires a valid licence from the National Lottery Regulatory Commission. The NLRC enforces strict standards around player protection, fair gaming, and financial transparency. All the bookmakers highlighted in this guide hold active NLRC authorisation, which means they undergo regular audits and contribute to national responsible gambling initiatives.
