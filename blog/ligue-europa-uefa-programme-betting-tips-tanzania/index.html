Licensing and regulation in Tanzania

All the bookmakers listed operate under a licence issued by the Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT). The board monitors operators for fair play, responsible gambling and anti‑money‑laundering compliance. A GBT licence guarantees that the bookmaker’s software is tested by independent auditors, that player funds are kept in segregated accounts and that any dispute can be escalated to a national regulator. When you see “GBT Licensed” on a bookmaker’s site, you can be confident that the operator meets Tanzanian legal standards.

Responsible gambling

Betting should be treated as entertainment, not a source of income. The Gaming Board of Tanzania requires every licensed operator to provide tools such as deposit limits, self‑exclusion and reality checks. If you feel your betting is becoming a problem, use the “Responsible Gaming” link in the bookmaker’s footer to set a weekly limit or to close your account temporarily. Local charities such as the Tanzania Mental Health Association also offer counselling services for gambling‑related issues. Remember, the excitement of the Europa League is best enjoyed when you stay in control of your bankroll.
