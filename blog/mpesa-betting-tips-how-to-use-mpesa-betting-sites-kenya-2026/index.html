M-Pesa Betting Guide — Kenya 2026

M-Pesa is Kenya's undisputed mobile money king, with over 30 million active users relying on it for everything from paying bills to funding their sports betting accounts. For Kenyan bettors, M-Pesa is the fastest, most secure, and most convenient way to deposit and withdraw at licensed betting sites. This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know about using M-Pesa for betting in Kenya.

Why M-Pesa is the Top Choice for Kenyan Bettors

M-Pesa's dominance in Kenya's betting scene isn't accidental. Here's why it's the preferred payment method:

  • Instant Deposits: Funds reflect in your betting account immediately after confirming the transaction.
  • Wide Acceptance: Every BCLB-licensed bookmaker in Kenya accepts M-Pesa, including giants like Betway, SportPesa, and 1xBet.
  • Security: M-Pesa uses PIN protection and transaction confirmations, making it safer than traditional banking methods.
  • Convenience: Accessible via USSD (*334#), the M-Pesa app, or directly on betting sites through STK Push.
  • Low Fees: Most bookmakers waive deposit fees, and withdrawal fees are minimal (typically KSh 10–50).

How to Deposit with M-Pesa at Kenyan Betting Sites

Method 1: USSD (*334#)

The classic method, ideal for feature phones or when internet is unavailable:

  1. Dial: *334# on your Safaricom line
  2. Select: Lipa na M-Pesa → Pay Bill
  3. Enter Business Number:

- Betway: 880084

- SportPesa: 880100

- 1xBet: 570420

  1. Enter Account Number: Your phone number or betting ID
  2. Enter Amount: Minimum KSh 50–100 (varies by site)
  3. Confirm with PIN: Funds reflect instantly

Method 2: M-Pesa App

Slightly faster and more user-friendly:

  1. Open M-Pesa App → Tap "Pay"
  2. Select Paybill: Enter the bookmaker's business number
  3. Account Number: Your phone number or betting ID
  4. Amount: Minimum KSh 50–100
  5. Confirm: Use PIN or fingerprint

Method 3: STK Push (Auto-Redirect)

The most seamless option, available on most modern betting sites:

  1. Go to Deposit: Select M-Pesa
  2. Enter Amount: Confirm on the betting site
  3. Receive Prompt: A payment request appears on your phone
  4. Enter PIN: Funds credited instantly

Troubleshooting Common M-Pesa Betting Issues

Deposit Not Reflecting?

  1. Check SMS: Ensure you received a confirmation from M-Pesa
  2. Wait 10 Minutes: Delays occasionally occur
  3. Contact Support: Provide the transaction ID (e.g., "RFP1234567")

Withdrawal Delayed?

  1. Verify Phone Number: Must match your M-Pesa account
  2. Complete KYC: Upload ID and proof of address
  3. Check Limits: Ensure you're within daily withdrawal caps

FAQ

How do I deposit with M-Pesa at Kenyan betting sites?

Use Lipa na M-Pesa (Pay Bill) or STK Push. Enter the bookmaker's business number, your account details, and confirm with your PIN.

Which bookmaker processes M-Pesa withdrawals fastest?

Betway and SportPesa typically process withdrawals within 15–30 minutes.

Are there fees for M-Pesa betting transactions?

Most deposits are free. Withdrawal fees range from KSh 10–50, depending on the amount.

Can I use M-Pesa if I'm not in Kenya?

M-Pesa is available in Tanzania, Ghana, and other African countries, but Kenyan betting sites require a Safaricom Kenya SIM.
