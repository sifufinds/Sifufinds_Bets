Licensing and Regulation in Zambia

The Gaming and Lotteries Board (GLB) is the sole regulator for betting activities in Zambia. Any operator displaying a GLB licence must submit regular financial reports, adhere to anti‑money‑laundering (AML) standards and protect player data under the Data Protection Act. The board also enforces responsible gambling policies, requiring operators to offer self‑exclusion tools and limit betting amounts for vulnerable users. When you see “GLB Licensed” next to a bookmaker’s name, you can be confident the platform is monitored by the national authority.

Secure Payment Methods for Zambian Bettors

Zambia’s betting market is heavily driven by mobile money. MTN MoMo Zambia and Airtel Money Zambia dominate because they allow instant deposits and withdrawals without a bank account. Zamtel Mobile Money is also gaining traction, especially in rural areas. For those who prefer traditional banking, Bank Transfer offers a reliable, though slower, route. Visa cards are accepted by most GLB‑licensed apps, giving an additional layer of security through card tokenisation. When selecting a payment method, look for two‑factor authentication (2FA) and encryption certificates on the app’s payment page. Avoid platforms that only accept crypto or unverified e‑wallets, as these are often unregulated.

Red Flags and Unlicensed Operators

Even with a GLB licence, some operators may still pose risks. Be wary of sites that:

  • Lack a visible licence number or display a licence from a jurisdiction outside Zambia, such as Curaçao, without also holding a GLB licence.
  • Require unusually high minimum deposits or charge hidden fees on withdrawals.
  • Offer “no‑wager” bonuses that sound too good to be true – these are usually a lure for unregulated operators.
  • Do not provide clear contact information or a physical address in Zambia.

If any of these signs appear, it is safer to walk away and choose a verified GLB‑licensed alternative.

Responsible Gambling

Betting should remain a form of entertainment, not a source of financial strain. All GLB‑licensed apps are required to display responsible gambling tools, including deposit limits, self‑exclusion periods and links to counselling services such as the Zambian National Suicide Prevention Hotline. Set a weekly budget that you can afford to lose, stick to it and never chase losses. If you notice signs of problem gambling – sleeplessness, anxiety or neglecting work – use the app’s self‑exclusion feature or seek professional help immediately.
