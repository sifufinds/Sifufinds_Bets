Gambling Taxes in Ireland: A Warning for African Markets?

Ireland is considering increasing its gambling tax rates, as reported by SBC News. The betting industry there is pushing back, but if this move goes through, it could set a precedent for other regions, including African countries.

For African bettors, this is a reminder to stay informed about local regulations. Countries like Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa already have varying tax structures for gambling. Any changes could impact the odds and promotions offered by bookmakers like 1xBet and Betway. Always compare markets across different platforms to get the best value.

Gamification Under Scrutiny: Are Gambling-Like Mechanics Safe?

GamCare, a UK gambling harms charity, has raised concerns about gamification in non-gambling sectors, as highlighted by SBC News. The question is whether these mechanics, often found in apps and services, are leading to harmful behaviors.

For African mobile betting users, this is a crucial issue. With the rise of platforms like Betika and Melbet, gamification is becoming more common. While it can make betting more engaging, it’s important to bet responsibly. Set limits, and don’t let flashy features distract you from your budget.

Betting Exchanges Decline in the UK: What Does This Mean for Africa?

The UK Gambling Commission’s 2025/26 Annual Report shows a sharp decline in betting exchange activity, with exchanges now making up just 3.79% of the market. This contrasts with the booming prediction markets in the US.
