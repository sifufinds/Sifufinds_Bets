The Ripple Effect of Other Groups

Scotland’s fate does not exist in a vacuum. The results of Group B (Brazil, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Qatar, and the third‑placed team) will dictate the number of points needed. Conversely, a surprise win by Qatar would see Bosnia’s points drop, potentially allowing a lower‑placed team to claim a second‑place slot and freeing up a “best third‑placed” berth for Scotland if they manage a win.
