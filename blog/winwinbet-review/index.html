Licensing: Curaçao Is Not Permission to Operate in Your Country

An offshore Curaçao licence is common among international bookmakers. It is not the same as permission to take bets in your country.

Check the local regulator before you register. In Ghana that is the Gaming Commission. In Kenya it is the Gambling Regulatory Authority. In Nigeria, start with the National Lottery Regulatory Commission and your state regulator. Ethiopia needs particular care, because our own Ethiopia page notes that sports betting licences were revoked in 2024 and the market is restricted.

We found no local licence for WinWinBet in any of these markets. That does not prove it is unlicensed, only that we could not find it. Do not rely on a badge in the site footer. Ask support for the licence number and check it with the issuing body.
