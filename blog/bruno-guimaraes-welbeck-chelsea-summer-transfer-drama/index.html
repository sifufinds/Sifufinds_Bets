FAQ

Q1: What happens to my bet if a transfer falls through after I place a wager?

Most bookmakers will void the market and refund stakes if the underlying event (the transfer) does not occur. Always check the specific “Transfer Completion” clause in the bet terms.

Q2: Are there special promotions for new signings?

Bet9ja and Betway frequently run “Free Bet” or “Deposit Boost” offers when a high‑profile player joins a club. Keep an eye on the promotions page before you place your stake.

Q3: How can I hedge my position on a volatile market like Arsenal title odds?

You can place a lay bet on a betting exchange (e.g., Betfair) or use a “double‑chance” market (e.g., Arsenal win or finish top‑2) to protect against sudden price swings.

Q4: Do African bookmakers offer live streaming for Premier League matches?

Yes, Bet9ja, Sportybet and 1xBet provide live streaming for most PL fixtures, which is handy when you want to follow a debut (like Welbeck’s first Chelsea appearance).
