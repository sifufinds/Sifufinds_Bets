Licensing and Regulation

In South Africa, online betting is regulated by the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB), and the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB). All licensed bookmakers must comply with strict regulations to ensure fair play and protect consumer rights. This regulatory framework helps to create a safe and secure betting environment for players in South Africa.
