Licensing and regulation in Ethiopia

Betting activity in Ethiopia falls under the jurisdiction of the National Lottery Administration (NLA). While the NLA does not issue licences to foreign operators directly, it requires that any bookmaker targeting Ethiopian customers complies with international gambling standards and provides a clear avenue for dispute resolution. Responsible gambling in Ethiopia

Betting should always be treated as entertainment, not a source of income. The National Lottery Administration promotes responsible gambling and requires licensed operators to provide tools such as deposit limits, self‑exclusion and reality‑check reminders. If you feel you are betting more than you can afford, use the “Self‑Exclusion” feature on your bookmaker’s site or contact the NLA’s helpline for advice.

Set a weekly betting budget in Ethiopian birr and stick to it. Never chase losses, especially when the game is high‑stakes like a college football showdown.
