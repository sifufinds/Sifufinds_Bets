Roland Garros Betting Strategy

  1. Clay specialists outperform — Players with 40%+ clay win rates historically outperform their general ranking suggests at Roland Garros
  2. Beware upsets in rounds 1–3 — High-ranked players often drop a set or two in early rounds while adjusting to conditions
  3. Weather matters — Heavy rain can dramatically affect ball behaviour. Check Paris weather before live betting
  4. In-play value — Early breaks in clay matches often don't hold. Lay heavy favourites after an early break for value
