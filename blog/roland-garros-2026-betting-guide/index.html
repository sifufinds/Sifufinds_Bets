Roland Garros 2026: Your Complete Betting Guide

The French Open is the most prestigious clay court Grand Slam, and 2026 promises to be the most competitive in years. Here's everything African tennis bettors need to know.

The Favourites

Carlos Alcaraz (2.80) – The Spaniard is the natural clay court favourite. Three-time Roland Garros champion. His movement and topspin are tailor-made for the Parisian red clay.

Jannik Sinner (3.20) – The world number 1 has transformed his clay game. Won the Monte Carlo Masters and Rome Open in the lead-up. Dangerous on this surface now.

Novak Djokovic (8.00) – Past his peak but mentally unbreakable. At 38, a miracle run is always possible on his favourite Grand Slam surface historically.

Women's Draw

Iga Świątek (1.90) – The Queen of Clay. Five Roland Garros titles. She's often shorter than 2.00 and represents genuine value if you can find her at that price.

Aryna Sabalenka (4.50) – The world number 2 has improved dramatically on clay but still faces questions about her Roland Garros ceiling.

Betting Markets Explained

  • Outright Winner – Best value in early rounds when odds are freshest
  • Set Betting – Alcaraz 3-0 in early rounds pays well (1.80-2.20)
  • Match Total Games – Over 36.5 for competitive matches is a reliable market
  • First Set Winner – Good for in-play if you have live streaming access
