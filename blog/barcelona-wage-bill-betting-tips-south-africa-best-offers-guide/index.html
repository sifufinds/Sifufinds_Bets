Licensing, regulation and consumer protection in South Africa

Betting in South Africa is regulated at both national and provincial levels. The primary regulator is the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), which licences operators such as Hollywoodbets and BetXchange. The National Gambling Board (NGB) oversees brands like Supabets and FairPari South Africa. Provincial gambling boards – for example the Eastern Cape Gambling Board (ECGBB) – also enforce compliance in their jurisdictions. All licensed operators must adhere to strict anti‑money‑laundering (AML) and responsible‑gaming standards, providing players with tools to set deposit limits, self‑exclude and access support services.

Because the licences are issued by reputable South African bodies, bettors enjoy a high level of consumer protection. When choosing a bookmaker, always verify that the site displays its licence number and the issuing authority.
