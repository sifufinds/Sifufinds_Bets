Licensing and Regulation in Tanzania

The Gaming Board of Tanzania (GBT) is the official regulator overseeing all betting activities in the country. Bookmakers licensed by GBT adhere to strict standards regarding player protection, fair play, and responsible gambling. Betting with GBT-licensed operators guarantees that your bets are secure and that the bookmaker operates within Tanzanian law.

Payment Methods Popular in Tanzania

Mobile money dominates Tanzania’s payment landscape. M-Pesa (Vodacom) is the most widely used, followed by Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, and Halotel Pesa.
