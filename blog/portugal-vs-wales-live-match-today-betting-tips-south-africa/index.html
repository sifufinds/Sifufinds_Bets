Licensing and regulation in South Africa

South Africa’s gambling landscape is overseen by three provincial bodies: the Western Cape Gambling and Racing Board (WCGRB), the National Gambling Board (NGB) and the Eastern Gauteng Casino and Betting Board (ECGBB). Each regulator enforces strict licensing standards, responsible gambling measures and anti‑money‑laundering protocols. Bookmakers operating under a WCGRB or NGB licence must submit regular audit reports, maintain transparent odds calculations and provide a clear grievance procedure. When you choose a sportsbook, verify that its licence is displayed on the footer of the site and that the regulator’s name matches one of the three bodies listed above.
