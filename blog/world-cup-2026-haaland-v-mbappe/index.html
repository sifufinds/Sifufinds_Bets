The 2026 World Cup Factor

The 2026 World Cup, co-hosted by the USA, Canada, and Mexico, introduces a new format with 48 teams, divided into 12 groups of 4. This means more matches, more upsets, and more opportunities for African teams like Senegal, Morocco, and Nigeria to make deep runs. For Haaland and Mbappé, the expanded format could mean tougher knockout opponents earlier in the tournament.

Format Changes:

  • Group Stage: 3 matches per team
  • Knockout Stage: Top 2 from each group + 8 best third-placed teams

This format favors consistent goal-scorers like Haaland and Mbappé, but it also increases the risk of early exits for top teams.
