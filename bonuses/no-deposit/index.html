What Is a No Deposit Betting Bonus?

A no deposit bonus is a promotional offer that gives new customers free bet credit or free spins without requiring any payment. You simply register an account, complete any required verification, and the bonus is credited automatically.

No deposit bonuses are common in Africa at sportsbooks like BetKing (Nigeria), Betika (Kenya, Ghana, Tanzania, Uganda), and Hollywoodbets (South Africa, Namibia, Mozambique, Botswana).

Types of no deposit bonuses:

  • Free bets — A fixed amount (e.g. ₦100, KSh 200) to bet with. Winnings are yours; the stake is not returned.
  • Bonus credit — Credited to your account balance, subject to wagering requirements.
  • Free rounds (Aviator/virtual) — Common in Nigeria. A set number of free goes on crash or virtual games.
