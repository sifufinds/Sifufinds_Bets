How to Withdraw Betting Winnings — Step by Step

1

Log into your betting account

Open the bookmaker's app or website. Log in with your username and password.

2

Go to Withdrawal or Cash Out

Navigate to My Account, Wallet, or Balance section. Click Withdraw or Cash Out.

3

Select your payment method

Choose M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, OPay, or bank transfer — the same method you used to deposit. Most bookmakers only allow withdrawal to the same payment method used for deposit.

4

Enter the amount and confirm your details

Enter the withdrawal amount (above the minimum). Confirm your mobile number or bank account details. Check for any fees.

5

Submit the withdrawal request

Click Confirm or Withdraw. You may receive an SMS or OTP to verify. The request is sent to the bookmaker's payment team.

6

Wait for processing

Mobile money withdrawals arrive in 5–30 minutes. Bank transfers take 1–3 days. If your withdrawal is pending for over 24 hours, contact customer support.

Common Withdrawal Problems & Solutions

⚠️ Withdrawal pending over 24 hours? Contact the bookmaker's customer support via WhatsApp, email, or phone. Have your account ID, withdrawal amount, and transaction reference ready.
  • KYC/Identity verification required — Most bookmakers require ID verification before processing your first large withdrawal. Submit a photo ID via the bookmaker's app.
  • Active wagering requirements — If you claimed a bonus, you may need to meet the wagering requirements before withdrawing. Check the bonus T&Cs in your account.
  • Wrong payment details — Double-check your mobile money number or bank account details before submitting.
  • Minimum not met — Check the minimum withdrawal amount for your chosen method (see table above).
  • Different deposit/withdrawal method — Most bookmakers require you to withdraw to the same method you deposited with (anti-money laundering rule).
💡 Pro tip: For fastest withdrawals, use mobile money (M-Pesa, OPay, MTN MoMo) rather than bank transfer. These process in minutes rather than days.
