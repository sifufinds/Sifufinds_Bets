What Is an Accumulator Bet?

An accumulator (also called an ACCA, multi, or combo bet) is a single bet that combines two or more match selections. The odds from each selection are multiplied together, creating significantly higher potential returns than single bets.

The catch: every selection in your accumulator must win. If even one selection loses, the entire bet loses.

Selection 1: Nigeria to beat Ghana @ 2.10
Selection 2: Senegal to win vs Cameroon @ 1.80
Selection 3: Morocco to beat Tunisia @ 1.75
Selection 4: Egypt to win vs Algeria @ 1.90
Combined Odds: 2.10 × 1.80 × 1.75 × 1.90 = 12.57

Tips for Building Winning Accumulators

  • Don't add selections just to increase odds. Every extra selection multiplies risk. 3-4 strong picks beat a weak 8-fold every time.
  • Stick to markets you understand. Home win, over/under, and BTTS are more predictable than correct score or first goalscorer.
  • Check for ACCA boost eligibility. Use bookmakers with boost offers (Bet9ja, 1xBet) when building accumulators — free extra money on the same bet.
  • Use SifuFinds free tips. Our daily predictions identify high-confidence ACCA legs across AFCON, CAF CL, and EPL.
  • Set a budget and stick to it. Accumulators are high-variance. Never stake more than you can afford to lose on a single bet.
⚠️ Important: Accumulators are exciting but statistically challenging. The more selections you add, the harder it is to win. Always use them as entertainment — never bet with money you need.

FAQ — Accumulator Betting

An accumulator (ACCA) is a single bet combining multiple match selections. All must win. Odds are multiplied together — a 4-fold at 2.0 odds each = 16.0 combined odds. High risk, but big potential returns from small stakes.

Multiply all selections' odds together, then multiply by your stake. Example: 4 selections at 2.0 × 1.8 × 1.9 × 2.1 = 14.36. A ₦1,000 stake returns ₦14,360 total (₦13,360 profit). Use our free tips page to find high-confidence ACCA legs.

An ACCA boost adds extra winnings to your accumulator. For example, Bet9ja Nigeria offers up to 170% boost on 3+ legs. If your acca returns ₦10,000, a 170% boost adds ₦17,000 — total ₦27,000. Boost percentages increase with more legs.

If any selection loses, the entire accumulator loses. This is unlike system bets (like Trixie or Lucky 15) which pay out partial wins. Some bookmakers offer ACCA Insurance — they refund your stake if one leg loses. Check each bookmaker's promotions page.
