Responsible Gambling

Support, tools and helplines for safer betting

Home›Responsible Gambling
⚠️ Gambling involves risk. Never bet more than you can afford to lose. If gambling is affecting your life, seek help immediately.

Getting Help

📞 GamCare: 0808 8020 133 (UK) · BeGambleAware: begambleaware.org · NCPG Africa: ncpgafrica.org

These organisations offer free, confidential support to anyone worried about their own or someone else's gambling. You do not need to have a diagnosed problem to reach out — early support prevents bigger problems later.

Signs of Problem Gambling

  • Betting with money needed for essentials — rent, food, bills
  • Chasing losses by placing bigger bets
  • Hiding gambling activity from family or friends
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or depressed when not gambling
  • Borrowing money to gamble

Self-Help Tools

Most licensed African bookmakers offer deposit limits, session time limits, self-exclusion and cooling-off periods. Contact your bookmaker's responsible gambling team to activate these tools — most process the request within 24 hours.

You can self-exclude from multiple bookmakers at once via your country's gambling regulator — the NLRC in Nigeria, BCLB in Kenya, GCA in Ghana, or the WCGRB in South Africa.

Safe Gambling Tips

  • Set a strict weekly budget before you start
  • Never chase your losses
  • Treat betting as entertainment, not income
  • Take regular breaks
  • Never bet under the influence of alcohol
