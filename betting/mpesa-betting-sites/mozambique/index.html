What is M-Pesa?

M-Pesa is East and Southern Africa's leading mobile money platform. Launched by Safaricom in Kenya in 2007, it now operates across 7 African countries. Bettors use M-Pesa USSD (*334# in Kenya) or the M-Pesa app to deposit and withdraw instantly at licensed bookmakers.

How to Deposit with M-Pesa at a Betting Site in Mozambique

  1. Go to M-Pesa on your phone (dial *334# or use the app)
  2. Select "Lipa na M-Pesa" → "Pay Bill"
  3. Enter the bookmaker's business number
  4. Enter your betting account number as the reference
  5. Enter the amount and your M-Pesa PIN
  6. Your betting account is credited instantly
