What is Telebirr?

Telebirr is Ethiopia's national mobile money platform, operated by Ethio Telecom. Launched in 2021, it has grown rapidly to over 40 million users, making Ethiopia one of Africa's fastest-growing mobile money markets. Telebirr is now the primary payment method for sports betting in Ethiopia.
