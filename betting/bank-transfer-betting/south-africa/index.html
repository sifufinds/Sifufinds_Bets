What is Bank Transfer?

Bank transfer is a widely accepted payment method at licensed African bookmakers. While slower than mobile money, bank transfers often allow larger deposit and withdrawal amounts. They are particularly popular for high-volume bettors in Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, and Ghana.

How to Deposit with Bank Transfer at a Betting Site in South Africa

  1. Log in to your online banking or mobile banking app
  2. Select "Transfer" or "Pay a Bill"
  3. Add the bookmaker as a payee with their account details
  4. Enter the transfer amount
  5. Use your betting account number as the reference
  6. Confirm the transfer — credited within 1-24 hours
