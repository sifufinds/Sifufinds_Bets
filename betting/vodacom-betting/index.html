How to Deposit via Vodacom M-Pesa in Tanzania

  1. 1. Register at a GBT-licensed bookmaker — Sign up at Sportybet Tanzania, 1xBet Tanzania, or Betway Tanzania. All accept Vodacom M-Pesa.
  2. 2. Select M-Pesa (Vodacom) deposit — In the Deposit section, choose M-Pesa and enter your Vodacom Tanzania phone number and deposit amount.
  3. 3. Dial *150*00# on your Vodacom line — Enter your M-Pesa PIN to confirm. The transaction is processed via Vodacom's M-Pesa platform.
  4. 4. Start betting — Funds are available instantly. Bet on the Tanzania Premier League, AFCON, or the Premier League.
