Methodology

How We Review and Rate Bookmakers

Our editorial team evaluates every bookmaker across six dimensions before it appears on SifuFinds. No bookmaker can pay for a higher rating — all scores reflect our independent assessment.

🛡️Step 1

Licence Verification

We confirm the operator holds a valid licence from the relevant national regulator before listing. Unlicensed operators are never listed.

💰Step 2

Bonus Accuracy

We verify every bonus claim directly with the operator. Bonus amounts, wagering requirements, and expiry dates are checked and updated daily.

💳Step 3

Payment Methods

We confirm which local mobile money and banking options are accepted. We test deposit and withdrawal processes for each operator.

📱Step 4

Mobile & App Quality

We test the mobile experience on Android and iOS, including load speed, usability, and in-play betting functionality.

💬Step 5

Customer Support

We assess response times and quality via live chat, WhatsApp, and phone where available. All in local business hours.

🏆Step 6

Market Coverage

We count available sports and markets, with particular attention to African leagues: NPFL, KPL, Ghana Premier League, PSL, and CAF competitions.
