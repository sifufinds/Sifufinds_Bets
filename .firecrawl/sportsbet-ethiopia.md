The first step is selecting a trusted and legal sports betting site. Always look for sportsbooks that are licensed either locally or by reputable international bodies. Once you’ve selected a sportsbook, you’ll need to register. This typically involves providing your phone number, full name, and sometimes your kebele ID number. #### 3\. **Make a Deposit**

Most sports betting platforms in Ethiopia support local mobile payment systems like **Telebirr**, **HelloCash**, and **M-Birr**. These methods are fast, secure, and commonly used across the country. New users might want to start with simpler bets while they get familiar with the system. Most platforms offer tutorials or betting tips to help beginners make informed choices.

While sports betting in Ethiopia is legal and increasingly popular, it’s important to bet responsibly.
